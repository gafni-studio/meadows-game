# Meadows v0.2.7 — Paths

The first step of the single-class transition. Keep this release **minimal**: open the Path system, put the existing Warrior skills on it, ship the tree UI and a tutorial, and fix a short list of polish items. More skills and branches are added later, both horizontally and vertically. Design only; the developer has room to interpret.

**Still in force:** one playable character. Soul Guard, the Meadow Shrine, infusion, Spiritual, and Hunter’s Luck stay parked. Gear drops stay very rare. Recipe rules from v0.2.6 still apply: materials from mobs near the pace of that rank, amounts about 10–40, the family’s essence is the main cost. No exhaustive tables here — use those rules and the pacing guide below.

---

## Hero, and Paths

The character is a single class: **Hero**. Every player-facing use of “Warrior” (character screen, creation, journal, dialogue, tutorials) becomes “Hero.”

**Paths** are open skill trees a Hero can train. The **Warrior Path** is the first and only train-able path in this release. The **Magician Path** arrives in v0.2.8 — the UI and data should already allow more than one path (a second tab is enough; it can read “coming soon”).

**Rules**

- Any Hero can train any path (only Warrior is filled in this release).
- To learn or raise a skill, the player needs the **previous skill in its chain** (at least rank 1) plus the materials. There is **no level gate**. Levels are only a pacing guide for recipe cost: **green ~ level 5**, **blue ~ level 10**, **orange ~ level 20**. Orange is out of scope for this release.
- Each family’s essence is the main cost for that family’s skills: Nature Essence for Nature, Elemental Essence for Elemental. Spirit skills are left out for now (see Future slots).
- Going deep in one path should beat splitting: a player who invests down one branch ends up stronger than one who spreads the same materials across many skills at low rank.
- Equip slots: **about 3–4 attack slots and 2 buff slots**. These are part of the new slot work (see Tree UI).

## The Warrior Path

Use only **existing** skills, chained as in Oded’s drawing. **No new Warrior skills** in this release.

```
Power Strike (basic, root)
  ├── Earthbreaker (Nature)
  │     ├── Root Lash (Nature)
  │     └── Flame Cleave (Elemental)
  ├── Dig In (Nature)
  └── Rage (basic)
```

- **Power Strike** is the root (basic). It stays the first skill, learned through The Weight of a Swing.
- From Power Strike, three branches: **Earthbreaker** (Nature), **Dig In** (Nature), **Rage** (basic).
- From Earthbreaker: **Root Lash** (Nature) and **Flame Cleave** (Elemental).

Existing quests that introduce these skills (The Weight of a Swing, Blood Up, A Crack in the Dirt, Hot Edge, and Dig In’s current unlock) can stay; they now also satisfy “previous skill in the chain.” Recipes are rebalanced to the pacing guide (green for the early branches, blue for Flame Cleave’s later ranks). Example: Earthbreaker rank 1 stays a green, Banks-paced Nature Essence recipe in the 10–40 band, as in v0.2.6; Flame Cleave rank 1 is still Elemental Essence plus Banks fire and earth goods, not a huge Pasture grind. Do not bring back the old 1.6× scaling.

### Future slots (not in this release)

The drawing has further branches. Leave them visible as **empty future slots** on the tree, or simply omit them and note them for later — do not implement them now:

- A center line down from Power Strike.
- Follow-ups from Rage.
- **Soul Guard** (Spirit), down from Dig In — stays **parked**.
- A Spirit follow-up from Flame Cleave (e.g. a cursed blow) — future content.

No Spirit skills are trainable in v0.2.7.

## Path tree UI

This is a required piece of the release, not a later polish pass.

- A **visual tree per path**: nodes for each skill, **connecting lines** for the chain, readable at a glance.
- **Family colors** on the nodes or lines (basic, Nature, Elemental — and a Spirit color reserved for later).
- Three states, clearly different: **locked** (previous skill not yet learned), **available** (can be trained, materials permitting), **trained** (shows current rank).
- When a node is available or selected, show the **cost of the next unlock or rank**.
- **Tabs per path**, with Warrior filled and a Magician tab ready for v0.2.8.
- **Clean equip slots** on the same new work: 3–4 attack and 2 buff. Icons sit properly in the slot (no clipped or oddly scaled art). Labels do not wrap awkwardly. The current hotbar’s messy icons and wrapped names are what this replaces.

The player should be able to open the tree, see what they can train next, and equip what they have, without confusion.

## Warrior Path tutorial

A **short tutorial quest from Calden** that introduces Paths as a system. It should cover, in a few short conversations (and a look at the new tree):

- What a Path is, and that this character is a Hero walking the Warrior Path.
- **Families** (basic, Nature, Elemental) and that **essence** of that family is the main cost.
- How the chain works (you need the previous skill).
- **Passives** (point them at Ila and the existing Quiet Work / first-point flow; do not replace that quest).
- Core training mechanics: open the tree, see a locked vs. available node, train, equip a skill.

Fit it around current NPCs: Calden leads it, starting naturally after The Weight of a Swing (Power Strike is already known, so the tree has a root). Ila still owns passives; Pell still owns The Dirt Path. Keep it a short chain, not a new saga. Existing skill quests remain the way individual skills are discovered.

## Fixes and polish

- **Starting weapon** is a class-neutral **Club**, with the **same fighting numbers** as today’s Wooden Sword. Stone Club stays the later Dirt Banks piece; do not confuse the two. The Wooden Sword is no longer what a new Hero starts with.
- **Basic attack** uses a nice, responsive **swing / slash** animation (not the overhead smash). It should feel snappy, reach the real melee range, and still land damage on the impact frame, with the existing hit-stop, sparks and crit shake.
- **Pell’s first quest** (The Dirt Path) is missing its marker. When it is available, Pell shows `!`; when a step is ready to turn in, he shows `?` — the same rules as Calden, Mara and Ila.
- **XP:** Field Mouse is **4 XP** (was 7), Wool Sheep is **8 XP** (was 12). Other mobs are unchanged. Early leveling is slower (about 18 mice to level 2 at the current 70 XP for 1→2).
- **E toggles** NPC menus: if a shop, training, exchange or talk window is already open, E closes it, instead of only opening.
- “Warrior” → “Hero” everywhere the player can read it, as above.
