# Meadows v0.3.0 — The Split Meadows and Whisperwood

The first large content release. The Meadows get split into east and west halves with vertical levels, and a new zone opens past them: **Whisperwood**, a misty forest for levels 5–10, with a short story chain that builds to the **Hollow Stag**. This is design only, so the developer has room to interpret.

**Rules carried over from v0.2.9:**
- Quests unlock by level or prerequisite, and the journal shows only quests the player can start.
- Players gather their own quest materials. No NPC hands over kits.
- Quest rewards stay low, close to the v0.2.9 table.
- Drop rates follow v0.2.9. No item drops more than 3 per kill, except Copper.
- Markers stay `!` (available) and `?` (ready to turn in). Every completion plays the banner and then the rewards screen.

**Also unchanged:**
- Path chains and unlock order.
- Essence, Training Scroll and Copper amounts in every recipe. Only the other rank materials change (section 6).
- Hero HP and the XP curve.
- Spirit skills, Soul Guard, the Meadow Shrine and Frost Moth stay parked.

## 1. Map split: east and west, with vertical levels

Every outdoor area becomes two halves. Most halves get **2–3 vertical levels**, MapleStory style:
- **Going up:** ladders, ropes or vines, plus short jumps between stepped platforms.
- **Going down:** drop through a platform (down + jump), or simply fall.

**Layout rules:**
- Each level should be a real place: a few mobs, a reason to be there, and room to fight.
- Avoid long empty shafts. A climb should take a few seconds, not a trek.
- Mobs patrol their own level. They only change level by a ledge they can actually reach.
- An aggressive mob that can't reach the player gives up and returns to patrol, as in v0.2.6. This matters more with ladders.
- Each half has a portal at both ends. Town connects to Meadow West, which leads to Meadow East, then Dirt Banks West, then Dirt Banks East. Whisperwood branches north from Meadow East (see section 2).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Meadow West** (was the town side of Sunny Pasture) | 1–3 | **Flat.** Open grass, a fence and one low ledge at most. Stays the gentle starter field. | Field Mouse 8, Wool Sheep 6 | — |
| **Meadow East** | 3–7 (grove 10) | **Hilly.** Ground: rolling slopes. Middle: hill terraces linked by short ladders. Top: a raised grove on a rise, reached by a rope. | Ground: Crow 4. Terraces: **Meadow Boar 4** | **Wool-Shoulder** on the far ground (The Bargain of Rage). **Old Stump** (boss, level 10, daytime) in the raised grove. The Whisperwood road starts behind the grove. |
| **Dirt Banks West** (riverbank) | 4–5 | A shallow river along the bottom. Mud shelves above it, then high bank ledges tied together with root ladders. | Burrow Mole 6, Earth Sprite 5 | — |

- **New mobs in the Meadows:** only **Meadow Boar** (already in the database: level 7, 225 HP, 47 XP) and **Old Stump** (level 10 boss, the only Heartwood source). Use their existing stats and drop tables. Night Stump stays parked.
- The Old Stump is an optional blue-tier fight around level 10. Heartwood is not required by any recipe yet.

**Quest locations after the split:**
- Path of the Warrior mice and Wool Before Winter (wool and fur) → **Meadow West**.
- The Bargain of Rage (Wool-Shoulder) → **Meadow East** ground, far end.
- Beyond the Pasture Gate: crows in **Meadow East**, then walk into **Dirt Banks West**. Its hunt (Burrow Moles and Ember Beetles) spans both Banks halves; the objective line says where to find each.
- What the Banks Hide (Earth Grit, Stone Chunk) → **Dirt Banks West**.
- The Cracked Stone: reach **Dirt Banks West**, gather there, then the Bank-Cracker’s den in **Dirt Banks East**.
- Ember in the Scale → **Dirt Banks East**.
- Update objective text and the dialogue that names places. Pell’s lore lines can mention the river and the cave.

**Check:** walk from town to the far end of Dirt Banks East.
- Every half has its listed mobs and no others.
- Meadow West has no climbing.
- The other halves each have reachable upper and lower levels.
- Every current quest can still be finished where its text says.

## 2. Whisperwood (levels 5–10)

A misty forest **north of Meadow East**, past the raised grove. Light comes in shafts through the fog, fireflies drift, and the trees are old enough to walk on.

**Getting there:** the road opens with the first Whisperwood quest (level 5). Before that, the grove path shows *“The mist is too thick to follow. Pell might know the way.”*

**Wren’s camp:** a small safe camp at the forest edge. A new NPC, **Wren**, the woodwarden, stands there (no shop). Wren gives the Whisperwood chain. **Voice:** quiet, watchful, practical. Wren talks in tracks and weather.

**Layers.** One area, three layers, in this order of danger:

| Layer | Level | What it is | Mobs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Forest floor** | 5–8 | Moss, roots and dark bog pools. The entrance side is easier and the bog side is deeper and harder. | Wisp 6 (entrance side), Thornling 5, Mire Toad 4 (bog side) |
| **Canopy** | 7–9 | Branch platforms in two or three tiers, linked by vines and ropes. Gaps you jump. | Moss Lurker 5, Wisp 3 |
| **The Hollow** (clearing) | 10 | A ring of dead trees at the far end of the floor, reached through the bog. | **Hollow Stag** (miniboss) |

**Mob stats:**

| Mob | Level | HP | Attack | Defense | XP | Family | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wisp** | 5 | 130 | 25 | 7 | 30 | Spiritual | Passive. Drifts in slow loops and bobs on the air. Once hit, it fires slow, visible motes (ranged, like a Crow’s pebble), keeping its distance. |
| **Thornling** | 6 | 165 | 31 | 9 | 38 | Nature | Aggressive. A twiggy imp that hops in short jittery bursts and throws thorns from range. It closes in to scratch when cornered. |
| **Moss Lurker** | 7 | 240 | 36 | 13 | 50 | Nature | Ambush. It sits on canopy branches as a still mossy mound. It wakes only when the player comes within 3 tiles, then lunges with a bite (range about 30). Slow, heavy, hard to knock back. |
| **Mire Toad** | 8 | 270 | 40 | 14 | 60 | Nature | Passive. Big heavy hops. Its attack is a tongue lash (range about 44), telegraphed by a puffed throat. |
| **Hollow Stag** (miniboss) | 10 | 1,600 | 48 | 18 | 160 | Nature | Aggressive, stays inside the Hollow, respawns in 3 minutes. See below. |

**Affinity** (v0.2.6 rules):
- Nature mobs are weak to Elemental (Flame Cleave, Fire Bolt and Chain Spark hit at 1.5×).
- The Wisp (Spiritual) is weak to Nature (Earthbreaker, Root Lash and Thorn Volley hit at 1.5×).
- No Whisperwood mob is immune to anything.

**Speed and reach targets:**
- Thornling and Wisp are quick: about the Crow’s speed.
- The Moss Lurker is slow except during its lunge.
- The Mire Toad covers ground in hops, not walks.
- A same-level mob should take about 5–10% of the Hero’s HP per hit, as elsewhere.

### The Hollow Stag

A tall stag whose ribs are an empty hollow lit by a faint green moss-light. Moss hangs from its antlers. It is meant as a level-10, blue-tier fight: hard but fair for a prepared Hero with a few blue ranks.

**Moves** (each one clearly telegraphed):
- **Charge:** it lowers its head and paws the ground, then rushes across the clearing. Sidestep or jump it.
- **Antler sweep:** a wide arc in front of it.
- **Root stomp:** it rears up and stomps. A line of root spikes erupts along the ground. Jump it, or be on a ledge.

**Phases:**
- At 50% HP the hollow glows brighter and the mist thickens.
- The stomp then comes more often, and the charge chains into a sweep.
- Its speed rises only a little; the fight should get busier, not unfair.

**Fight target:** about 1–2 minutes for a level-10 Hero.

## 3. The Whisperwood chain

Five short quests, each unlocked by level or by the previous quest, following the v0.2.9 tone guide. Each has a start, a “not yet” and a turn-in. Rewards are low.

| # | Quest | Giver | Unlocks when | Asks | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Mist on the North Road** | Pell | Level 5, after Beyond the Pasture Gate | Climb to the raised grove in Meadow East, follow the north road and find Wren at the forest’s edge | 30 XP, 6 Copper |
| 2 | **Lanterns That Wander** | Wren | After quest 1 | Defeat 6 Wisps, bring 4 Wisp Dust | 40 XP, 8 Copper |
| 3 | **What Waits in the Branches** | Wren | Level 7, after quest 2 | Climb to the canopy, defeat 5 Moss Lurkers and 3 Thornlings, bring 4 Moss Fiber | 50 XP, 10 Copper, 1 Meadow Dew |
| 4 | **The Drowned Hollow** | Wren | Level 8, after quest 3 | Bring 3 Toad Gland from the Mire Toads, then follow the stag’s tracks to the Hollow | 55 XP, 10 Copper |
| 5 | **Antlers in the Mist** | Wren | Level 9, after quest 4 (aimed at level 10) | Defeat the Hollow Stag and bring Wren its Hollow Antler | 80 XP, 15 Copper, 3 Nature Essence |

### Copy

## 4. Pacing: blue at level 10, no orange

- **Level map:**
  - Meadow West 1–3, Meadow East ground 3–4.
  - Banks West 4–5, Banks East 4–6.
  - Whisperwood floor 5–8, canopy 7–9.
  - Meadow East terraces 7 (boars).
  - The Hollow and the Old Stump at 10.
- **Blue tier lands at level 10.** The Hollow Stag and the Old Stump are the blue-tier fights. Ranks 5–10 of every skill now ask for Whisperwood materials (section 6), so they are within reach of a Hero who hunts the wood from level 6 and the Hollow Stag at 10.
- No orange content: no mob above level 10, no orange recipes, no zone past Whisperwood.
- **XP target:** levels 5→10 take about 9,600 XP on the current curve. A Hero hunting level-appropriate Whisperwood mobs (30–60 XP each) needs on the order of 200 kills across the five levels, plus the chain’s small rewards. That keeps the Banks relevant up to about level 6 and the wood relevant through level 9.

## 5. Look and feel of every new mob

Every new mob and the Hollow Stag must feel finished, not placeholder.
- Each mob gets its own idle, move, attack wind-up, hit and death animation.
- Each has a readable silhouette against the fog, and its attacks are telegraphed.

**Wisp:**
- A soft glow that pulses and bobs on the air, trailing faint motes.
- It flickers and dims when hit.
- On death it dissolves into a spray of light.

**Thornling:**
- A jittery, twiggy hop.
- A clear arm-back wind-up before each thorn; the thorn is a readable green projectile.
- It bursts into leaves on death.

**Moss Lurker:**
- At rest, a breathing moss mound that is easy to miss on purpose.
- The ambush pops up and shakes off moss.
- The heavy lunge-bite has a short pause before it.
- It slumps back into moss on death.

**Mire Toad:**
- Its throat pulses at idle.
- It lands from a hop with a splash.
- Its cheeks puff before the tongue lash.
- On death it belly-flops with a big splash.

**Hollow Stag:**
- **Presence:** slow, heavy steps, mist curling off its back, and the moss-light breathing inside its ribs.
- **Telegraphs:** the head goes down before a charge, the body sways back before a sweep, and it rears tall before a stomp.
- **Impact:** a shockwave, screen shake and dust on every big hit.
- **Phase 2:** the glow brightens.
- **Death:** it kneels, then dissolves into mist and fireflies as the clearing brightens.

**Whisperwood itself:**
- Layered fog in parallax, shafts of light and drifting fireflies.
- Swaying vines on the canopy ropes, and ripples in the bog pools.

**Meadow East and the Banks halves:**
- Meadow East: wind in the hill grass.
- Banks West: water along the river.
- Banks East: drips and glints in the cave, and torchlit glow near the den.

## 6. Rank recipes follow the map

Players keep ranking up their existing skills, but a high rank should send them to where they are now, not back to Twigs and Bark in the starter field. This release rewrites the non-essence materials of every rank of the 11 existing skills. No new skills, no new ranks.

**Rules:**
- **Essence stays the main cost.** Each skill keeps its family's essence (Monster, Nature or Elemental) at today's amounts, plus today's Training Scroll and Copper. No other material in a rank asks for more than the essence.
- **Materials follow progression:**
  - **Ranks 1–2 (early):** commons from where the skill is learned. Meadow West and Meadow East ground (Fur, Wool, Feather, Bark, Dry Root) for skills learned in the Meadows. Skills learned at the Banks keep their Banks commons.
  - **Ranks 3–4 (mid, about level 5–7):** Dirt Banks and Meadow East materials: Stone Chunk, Earth Shard, Tough Root, Clay, Fire Shard, Lightning Shard, and the Meadow Boar's Leather Scrap, Copper Nail and Branch.
  - **Ranks 5–10 (late, paced at levels 6–10):** Whisperwood materials lead: Wisp Dust, Moss Fiber, Toad Gland. Each rank adds at most one Banks or Meadow East material as support. No Meadow West common appears from rank 3 onward.
- **Theme:** fire skills take Fire Shard and Toad Gland. Nature skills take Moss Fiber. Magic skills take Wisp Dust. Lightning takes Lightning Shard and Copper Nail. Physical skills take leather and Moss Fiber.
- **Earth Crystal** stays the rank 5 milestone (1) and appears again at rank 10 (2) for skills without an Antler.
- **Hollow Antler** (1) is reserved for rank 10 of four key skills, two per path: Earthbreaker and Flame Cleave (Warrior), Fire Bolt and Chain Spark (Magician). Those ranks take 1 Earth Crystal or none instead of 2.
- **Amounts:** Whisperwood materials ask for 6–18, because they drop at 25–30% in stacks of 1–3. Mid materials ask for 8–18.
- Players already above a rank are not affected; only future rank-ups use the new recipe.

**Example:** Fire Bolt rank 9 used to ask for 24 Fire Shard and 36 Earth Grit, both from level 4–6 Banks mobs. It now asks for 12 Toad Gland and 10 Wisp Dust, so a level-9 Hero farms the Whisperwood bog and floor.

**Check:**
- No rank from 3 up uses Twig, Bark, Fur, Wool, Feather or Pebble.
- Every rank from 5 up uses at least one Whisperwood material.
- Exactly four recipes use Hollow Antler.
