# Meadows v0.2.6 — Design Spec

This is the design brief for **v0.2.6**. It turns Oded's seventeen playtest notes (including 15b, 15c, and 15d) into decisions a developer can implement without guessing. Numbers are exact. Where something changes, it is written as **was X → now Y**, based on the live v0.2.5 game.

**Still in force from earlier releases:** Warrior is the only playable class. Soul Guard, the Meadow Shrine, infusion, Spiritual, and Hunter's Luck stay parked. Player-facing text stays short; longer design notes stay out of the player's way. Gear drop rates stay very rare (~1 in 150–200 kills). Quest gifts and shop stock are not drop-table changes. Monster Essence stays more common than category essence on every mob that drops both.

Do not invent a scaling formula for recipes. Use the tables in this document. Do not require Heartwood, Log, or Meadow Boar materials on any live recipe: the path east of the Dirt Banks is still closed, so those sources are not in play.

## 1. Quest rewards screen

At the moment a quest is turned in and completed, show a **dedicated rewards screen**. A toast is not enough.

**Layout (top to bottom):**

1. A clear heading: the quest's name, and the word **Complete**.
2. One short flavor line (the last line of the turn-in conversation is fine).
3. A **Rewards** list, each reward on its own row, in this order:
   - Experience points, as a number (for example `140 XP`).
   - Copper coins, as a number (for example `25 Copper`).
   - Each item, with quantity and the item's player-facing name (for example `6× Nature Essence`).
   - If the reward is gear, also show its exact stats on the same row (for example `1× Hide Vest — Defense 3`, `1× Stone Club — Weapon damage 16`).
   - If the quest grants a skill at rank 1, say so (`Power Strike rank 1`). If it only opens training, say that (`Flame Cleave training is now open`) — do not imply the skill was learned for free.
4. A single **Continue** control. Enter or a click dismisses the screen. The conversation's last page is finished before this screen appears (talk first, then rewards).

Quest gift gear is **exact and never magic**, using the typical (mid) column: Hide Vest is Defense 3; Stone Club is weapon damage 16 (multiplier 1.15, requires STR 5). Was: a mid roll that could land one point off (Stone Club was seen at 17). Now: always those exact values.

**Acceptance checks**

- Finish The Weight of a Swing: a rewards screen lists the XP, coins, and "Power Strike rank 1"; Enter closes it.
- Finish A Warm Coat: the screen lists 90 XP, 20 Copper, Hide Vest with Defense 3, and 4 Blueberries. The vest is never magic and always Defense 3.
- Finish What the Dirt Keeps: the screen lists 140 XP, 30 Copper, Stone Club with weapon damage 16, and 6 Nature Essence.
- Finish A Crack in the Dirt / Hot Edge: the screen says training is open, not that the skill was learned at rank 1, and still lists XP, coins, and items.
- No quest completes with only a toast and no screen.

## 2. Attack animations

Attack feel stays a priority. Hit-stop, crit screen-shake, hit sparks, and "damage lands on the impact frame, when the visual connects, at the actual skill or swing range" all stay as in v0.2.5. What changes is which motion belongs to which attack.

| Attack | Now |
| --- | --- |
| **Normal (basic) attack** | Uses the **old Power Strike smash**: a heavy overhead blow, weapon raised high and brought down, with an impact flash where it lands. The smash visual reaches the end of the actual basic-attack range (70). Damage on the impact frame. The v0.2.5 basic slash trail is replaced by this smash. |
| **Power Strike** | A **new forward axe swing**: the weapon comes from the rear hip, sweeps forward in a committed horizontal or diagonal cut, and follows through past the target. The body steps into the blow. A compact shock of dust and sparks at the far end of Power Strike's actual range. Impact flash on a hit. Damage on the impact frame, when the blade would connect. |
| **Earthbreaker** | **Unchanged.** The v0.2.5 ground crack and shockwave that travel the skill's full range. |
| **Root Lash** | **Unchanged.** Roots erupting from the dirt along the skill's range. |
| **Flame Cleave** | **New, better fire animation.** A wide, bright horizontal fan of fire that travels from the warrior to the far edge of Flame Cleave's actual range. Embers trail the arc. A bloom of fire pops on each enemy at the moment damage lands. A faint scorch lingers on the ground for a short moment. Damage is timed to when the fire front reaches the target, not to the key press. The fire must read clearly on grass and on dirt. |

Rage's red aura burst on cast is unchanged. Dig In's stamp is unchanged.

**Acceptance checks**

- A basic attack is an overhead smash (not a thin slash trail) that visibly reaches a target at the edge of melee range, with damage (or a miss) at the moment the smash lands.
- Power Strike is a forward axe cut, clearly different from the basic smash, with impact at the end of its range.
- Earthbreaker still cracks the ground and sends a shockwave along its range.
- Root Lash still erupts roots; it is not redesigned.
- Flame Cleave is a clear fire fan to the end of its range, with a bloom on hit, and damage timed to the fire arriving — visibly better than the v0.2.5 fire arc.
- Hit-stop, crit shake, and sparks still happen.

## 3. Quest markers above NPCs

Every NPC who can start or turn in a quest (Calden, Mara, Pell, Ila) shows a marker above their head.

- **`!`** — a new quest is **available**. Talking will start it (or play the opening conversation). The player meets any start conditions. Color: warm gold. A gentle bob so it is easy to see.
- **`?`** — a **turn-in is ready**. The current step's collect, kill, or reach objective is done, and talking will advance or complete the quest. Color: warm gold or a clear pale gold, distinct from `!` at a glance (a `?` shape is enough).
- **Nothing** — no quest, a step still in progress (you still need more items or kills), or a start the player cannot yet take (for example a level or stat gate that is not met, and no opening conversation is offered yet).
- If both would apply, **`?` wins** (turn in first).
- The marker disappears while the conversation is open, and updates as soon as the conversation ends or an objective is completed (picking up the last required Twig should raise a `?` on Calden without leaving the area).
- Visible from a short distance on the same screen; it sits clearly above the character, not inside their sprite.

**Acceptance checks**

- After The Weight of a Swing is done, Ila shows `!` for The Quiet Work. After you talk, the `!` is gone. After you have 8 Twig and 4 Bark, Ila shows `?`. After turn-in, the `?` is gone.
- Mara shows `!` for A Warm Coat only after The Quiet Work is done, and `?` when 8 Wool and 6 Fur are in the bag.
- Pell shows `!` for The Dirt Path once a first passive rank is spent, and `?` when a hunt or collect step is finished.
- Calden shows `?` when 4 Twig are ready on The Weight of a Swing, and no marker while you still have 0–3 Twig (unless another of his quests is ready).
- A gated "you're not ready" visit (Hot Edge before level 6 and 10 STR, if the quest is already in the journal) does **not** show `?`. It may show nothing, or a `!` only if talking starts a useful "not ready" conversation that the design of section 17 calls for — it must not look like a successful turn-in.

## 4. Open the objectives tracker from the journal

The on-screen objectives tracker (the panel already opened with **L**) can also be opened from the journal (**J**).

- The journal has a visible control labeled **Objectives (L)** (or equivalent, with the L hint).
- Clicking it shows the same tracker that L shows. Pressing L still works as it does today.
- The journal may stay open; the tracker is a heads-up list and should not be trapped behind the journal.
- Closing works the same as it does today for L (toggle off, or the tracker's own Hide).

**Acceptance checks**

- Open J, click Objectives (L): the tracker appears with the same quests and 3/8-style counts as pressing L.
- Pressing L still toggles the tracker when the journal is closed.
- The control is visible and labeled; a player does not have to guess.

## 5. Player HP

Lower starting HP and less HP per level. Strength's contribution is unchanged.

**Warrior (the only live class)**

| | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base (level 1, before STR) | 120 | **100** |
| Per level after 1 (before STR) | 10 | **7** |
| HP per STR | 2 | 2 (unchanged) |

Formula: Max HP = 100 + 7 × (level − 1) + STR × 2 (+ any magic-gear HP, unchanged).

Examples, before STR and gear: level 1 = 100 (was 120); level 5 = 128 (was 160); level 10 = 163 (was 210). A new Warrior who puts all 3 starting points into STR has 106 HP at level 1.

**Mage and Rogue** (not playable; recorded so the sheet stays consistent): Mage was 80 + 6 per level → **70 + 4**. Rogue was 100 + 8 → **85 + 6**. HP per STR stays 2. MP numbers are unchanged.

**Acceptance checks**

- A new Warrior at level 1 with 0 STR has 100 max HP; with 3 STR, 106.
- At level 5 with 0 STR, 128 max HP (not 160).
- At level 10 with 0 STR, 163 (not 210).
- Adding 1 STR still adds 2 max HP.
- MP, potions, and food heals are unchanged.

## 6. Shop gear is exact, and gear bonuses show on C

**Shop (Mara).** Every piece of gear she sells is a **fixed, identical item**. No random roll between a min and a max. Never magic. Every purchase of the same item is the same as every other.

| Item | Slot | Exact stats (now) | Was |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cloth Cap | Head | Defense **1** | Rolled 1–2 (typical 1) |
| Cloth Tunic | Torso | Defense **2** | Rolled 2–3 (typical 2) |
| Cloth Pants | Legs | Defense **1** | Rolled 1–2 (typical 1) |
| Cloth Shoes | Boots | Defense **1** | Rolled 1–2 (typical 1) |
| Cloth Gloves | Gloves | Defense **1** | Already always 1 |
| Short Sword | Weapon | Weapon damage **13**, multiplier 1.10, requires STR 1 | Rolled 12–14 (typical 13) |

A full cloth set is always Defense 6. The Short Sword's weapon contribution to attack is floor(13 × 1.10) = 14, the same as the old typical roll. Prices are unchanged (Cap 25, Tunic 35, Pants 30, Shoes 22, Gloves 22, Short Sword 30). Consumables she sells are already exact; they stay as they are.

Gear **drops** and their rare magic chance are unchanged (still very rare, still allowed to roll and to be magic). Quest gift gear is exact mid, never magic, as in section 1.

**Character screen (C).** Gear bonuses must be visible, not only baked into a total.

- The Attack and Defense totals stay, and they include gear.
- An **Equipment** section lists each equipped piece by name, with its bonuses: Defense +N, weapon damage N (and the multiplier for weapons), and every magic affix with its value (for example `+2 STR`, `+12 HP`).
- A short totals line: Defense from gear, attack from the weapon, and any STR / INT / AGI / SPI / HP / MP / accuracy / crit / regen coming from gear.
- The STR, INT, AGI, and SPI rows show the **total** the player actually has (base points plus gear). If gear contributes, say so next to the number (for example `STR 8  (+2 from gear)`).
- Empty slots can be omitted or marked empty; do not invent bonuses.
- Rage still appears on this screen only while the Rage buff is actually on (v0.2.5 rule, unchanged).

**Acceptance checks**

- Buy two Cloth Tunics in a row: both are Defense 2, neither is magic. Buy two Short Swords: both are weapon damage 13, neither is magic.
- Buy the full cloth set: Defense from those five pieces is exactly 6, every time.
- Open C with the cloth set and a Short Sword equipped: the Equipment list shows each piece and its defense or weapon damage; Attack and Defense totals match; no affixes appear (shop gear has none).
- Equip a magic drop that has, for example, +2 STR: C shows +2 STR on that piece, and the STR row includes it and says so.
- With Rage known but not active, C has no Rage row (v0.2.5 still holds).

## 9. Mob AI: give up, patrol, and wander

Mobs were getting stuck under a player who stood on a platform, and they wandered too little of the map.

**Wander (out of combat).** Idle mobs patrol a **wide** stretch of the walkable ground they spawn on — roughly a large share of their spawn stretch, not a tight circle around one point. They walk, pause briefly, choose a new direction, and repeat. They may hop up or down a platform they can actually reach. They stop at edges they cannot clear (they should not casually walk into fatal pits). Passive mobs still do not attack until hit. Aggressive mobs still aggro inside the existing 8-tile aggro range.

**Give up when stuck.** If a mob is aggroed but **cannot reach** the player for about **2.5 to 3 seconds** — the player is on a platform above, the mob is wedged under it, or it is repeatedly jumping into a ceiling or wall — it **drops aggro**, turns away, and returns to wander / patrol. It must not stand under the player swinging at the air, and it must not stay stuck forever. After giving up, it can **re-aggro** if the player comes back onto a surface the mob can reach, inside aggro range.

**Pathing when they can reach.** If there is a jump, drop, or walk they can actually make to the player, they take it. They are allowed to be persistent when the path is real.

**Leash** is unchanged (~20 tiles: they still go home if dragged that far).

**Acceptance checks**

- Stand on a Dirt Banks platform with a Burrow Mole aggroed below: within about 3 seconds it stops swinging, leaves, and patrols. It does not stay stuck under the platform.
- Drop down onto its platform: it can aggro again and chase.
- In the Pasture and the Banks, idle mobs walk a clearly wider stretch than a small spawn circle, including along their spawn stretch toward the sides of the area.
- A mole that can jump onto a low platform the player is on will do that, rather than giving up immediately.
- Dragging a mob far away still leashes it home.

## 10. Collection quests show progress (3/8, 0/6)

Every collect step and every hunt step shows **how many you have and how many you need**, in the form `3/8`, on both the **objectives tracker (L)** and the **journal (J)**.

- Collect: each required item is listed, for example `Twig 3/8 · Bark 1/4`. The count is what is in the bag, capped visually at the requirement (do not show `12/8`; show `8/8` once you have enough).
- Hunt: already shown as `Crow 2/4` (or similar); keep that, and use the same style everywhere.
- The number **updates live** when you pick up, spend, or turn in items, and when you get a kill.
- The objective sentence can keep its words; the fraction sits with it (for example `Bring Mara 8 Wool and 6 Fur (Wool 3/8 · Fur 0/6)`).

Quest requirements themselves (8 Twig, 4 Bark, 8 Wool, 6 Fur, 8 Earth Grit, 4 Stone Chunk, 6 Warm Grain, and the rest) are **unchanged**.

**Acceptance checks**

- The Weight of a Swing collect step shows `Twig 0/4` and becomes `Twig 4/4` when four are in the bag; L and J agree.
- The Quiet Work shows `Twig 3/8 · Bark 0/4` (or the same numbers) after picking up 3 Twig and 0 Bark.
- A Warm Coat and What the Dirt Keeps show both items the same way.
- A hunt step still shows `Crow 2/4` (and the other hunts).
- After turn-in consumes the items, the next step no longer shows the old 8/8.

## 11. Contact damage

Mobs that are **in combat** (aggressive from the start, or a passive mob that has already been aggroed) deal **contact damage** when their body touches the player.

- The hit is **40%** of a normal attack from that mob (same attack roll as a swing, then 40%, then the player's defense, then the usual minimum-damage rule). At least 1 damage if anything gets through.
- 40% is the chosen point inside Oded's 30–50% band.
- Contact is **physical** (no affinity bonus or immunity from section 16).
- Contact does **not** replace or consume the mob's swing; they can still wind up and strike normally. It is a bump for standing on them.
- The player's existing hurt invulnerability (~0.7 s) applies, so a touch, a swing, and another touch cannot stack in the same instant. At most one incoming hit (swing or contact) per invulnerability window.
- A small nudge is fine; do not use a full swing's knockback.
- **Passive** mobs that have not been aggroed (a peaceful Wool Sheep or Field Mouse you have not hit, an Earth Sprite you have not hit) do **not** deal contact damage. Once they are in combat, they do.
- Crow projectiles are still their ranged attack, not contact. Walking into a Crow that is in combat can contact-damage.

**Acceptance checks**

- Walk into an aggroed Burrow Mole without swinging: you take about 40% of a normal mole hit, then brief invulnerability, and you are nudged — not deleted.
- Walk into a peaceful Wool Sheep you have not hit: no damage. Hit it, then touch it: contact damage applies.
- Standing on an aggroed mob does not deal contact every frame; invulnerability spaces the hits.
- The mob can still perform a full swing for a normal (100%) hit after or before a contact bump, respecting the same invulnerability.

## 12. Skill presses on cooldown: no queue, mostly silent

Remove skill queueing entirely.

- If a skill is on cooldown (or otherwise not ready) and the player presses it, **nothing is queued**. The press is **ignored**.
- **No banner, no toast, no sound** for that press, except in one case: if **more than 5 seconds** remain on that skill's cooldown, show the existing "on cooldown" banner, and only on the first press of that unready stretch (not on a mash).
- In live numbers, that banner is for **Rage (60 s)** and parked **Soul Guard (30 s)** only. Power Strike (0.8 s), Earthbreaker (2.4 s), Flame Cleave (2.0 s), Root Lash (1.6 s), and Dig In (4.0 s) are all at or under 5 seconds — presses during those cooldowns are completely silent and do nothing.
- When the cooldown ends, the skill does **not** auto-fire. The player must press it again.
- Cooldown lengths themselves are unchanged from v0.2.5.

**Acceptance checks**

- Press Power Strike, then press it again immediately: it does not fire a second time when the cooldown ends; MP is spent only once; no "on cooldown" banner appears.
- Mash Power Strike during its cooldown: still only the first (successful) use, no banner, no queued shot.
- Press Rage during its 60 s cooldown: a single "on cooldown" banner; further mashes stay quiet; Rage does not fire when the 60 s ends unless pressed again.
- Dig In (4 s) is silent when re-pressed, same as Power Strike.

## 14. Junk items: remove about half of the high-rate clutter

High-rate **junk** means sell-only curios that are not used in any recipe, quest, or craft, and that drop often enough to flood the bag.

**Removed from every drop table** (about half of that high-rate set):

- Broken Twig
- Torn Cloth
- Lost Button
- Pasture Bell
- Cracked Shell

These five items may remain in the item list (existing bags can still hold and sell them at Mara). They simply no longer drop.

**Kept** (the other half of high-rate junk, still sellable at Mara): Old Bone, Fence Peg, Bright Bead, Warm Grain. Warm Grain is also a Hot Edge collect item — it must stay, and its drop is raised in section 15 so collecting 6 is fair.

**Acceptance checks**

- Warm Grain still drops from Ember Beetles (and Earth Sprites) for Hot Edge.
- Materials, essences, and gear drops are not removed by this cleanup.

## 15. XP curve, and more resources per drop

### 15.a XP: it was too fast from level 4

The climb from 4 to 8 (and on to 10) outleveled the Dirt Banks, and resource farming stopped mattering. From **level 4 onward**, it should take about **twice as many kills** to gain a level. Levels 1–3 stay as they are. Monster XP values are **unchanged** (a Field Mouse is still 7 XP, a Burrow Mole 23, an Earth Sprite 30, a Spark Bug 39, and so on).

**XP needed to go from level L to L+1**

| From | To | Was | Now | Rough kills at the matching mob (now) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 70 | **70** | ~10 Field Mice (7 XP) |
| 2 | 3 | 173 | **173** | ~14 Wool Sheep (12 XP) |
| 3 | 4 | 298 | **298** | ~18 Crows (17 XP) |
| 4 | 5 | 440 | **880** | ~38 Burrow Moles (23 XP) |
| 5 | 6 | 597 | **1194** | ~40 Earth Sprites (30 XP) |
| 6 | 7 | 768 | **1536** | ~39 Spark Bugs (39 XP) |
| 7 | 8 | 951 | **1902** | ~48 Spark Bugs, or mixed Banks hunting |
| 8 | 9 | 1145 | **2290** | Banks still relevant (next area is closed) |
| 9 | 10 | 1350 | **2700** | same |
| 10 | 11 | 1565 | **3130** | same |

- Levels 1–3: same formula as today, round(40 × L^1.5 + 30 × L).
- Level 4 and up: **twice that**, round(80 × L^1.5 + 60 × L).
- From 11 onward, keep using the "level 4 and up" formula.
- Combined with the Dirt Banks remaining the live hunting ground through the mid and late single digits (the east path is closed), this is what keeps the Banks relevant at levels 6–7, and keeps resource farming meaningful for rank 2 around 4–5 and rank 3 around 7–8.

Death penalty (5% of current-level progress) and all other XP rules are unchanged.

**Acceptance checks**

- Level 1→2 is still 70 XP; 2→3 is 173; 3→4 is 298. A Field Mouse still gives 7.
- Level 4→5 is 880 (not 440). 5→6 is 1194. 7→8 is 1902. 10→11 is 3130.
- A character at level 4 who earns 440 XP is only halfway to 5, not at 5.
- It takes on the order of 38 mole-worth of XP to go from 4 to 5, about twice the old ~19.

### 15.b–d Drop amounts, and recipes that match the player's level

Oded reached level 10 without being able to afford a skill at rank 2. Two things were wrong: XP was fast, and recipes scaled into the hundreds of low-level goods (Earthbreaker rank 4 was on the order of 140–200 Twigs, which only early pasture mobs drop).

**Rules for every live recipe (skills and passives):**

1. **No scaling formula.** Every rank has an explicit recipe in the tables below. Do not multiply rank 1 by 1.6.
2. **Amounts are about 10–40** per material, including essence and copper. Never hundreds. Milestone rares at ranks 5 and 10 are **1 or 2** of a rare, **in addition** to a normal 10–40 recipe — not instead of one.
3. **Materials come from mobs near the level the player is meant to be** for that rank. Rank 1 of early skills: the Pasture (and, for Earthbreaker, the Banks, which is where that skill opens). Rank 2: Dirt Banks, around level 4–5. Rank 3: still the Banks (Spark Bugs, Sprites, Beetles, Moles), around level 7–8. Ranks 4–10: higher Banks materials. **Low-tier pasture basics** (Twig, Bark, Fur, Wool, Feather, Pebble) appear **only in small amounts at ranks 1–2**, and not at all from rank 3 up.
4. **Targets:** a main combat skill is affordable at **rank 2 around level 4–5**, and **rank 3 around level 7–8**, after a realistic stint of hunting at that level (on the order of one level's worth of kills, ~38–40 Banks kills around level 4–5, with the new drop amounts).
5. **Passives are gated mainly by their own tree's essence** (Nature Essence for the Nature tree, Elemental Essence for the Elemental tree). Other materials are secondary, level-appropriate, and in smaller amounts. Copper is always in the recipe, in the 10–40 band.
6. **Heartwood, Log, and Meadow Boar-only goods are not on any live recipe.** Earth Crystal (Earth Sprites, 1.2%) is the Banks milestone rare at ranks 5 and 10. Fire Crystal stays a tiny flavor drop and is not required.

**Ila's kit** (The Quiet Work, given when 8 Twig and 4 Bark are turned in) must equal the **new** Nature production rank-1 recipe exactly, so she still funds the first point:

- Was: Pebble 50, Bark 40, Earth Shard 5, Nature Essence 50, Copper 30.
- Now: **Nature Essence 20, Pebble 16, Bark 12, Copper 18.**

Players who already received the old kit keep those items. New completions give the new kit. Her conversation should name the new items, not the old ones.

#### Resource drop policy (all mobs)

**Amounts of every resource go up** (typically about 1.5–2× the old stack, and the minimum often rises by 1). **Rates** from v0.2.5 stay, except where a rate bump is named below so a recipe or quest is actually farmable. Gear drop rates are **unchanged**. Removed junk is simply absent (see section 14). Full tables are in the **Full drop tables** section later; that section is authoritative.

Named rate bumps (and why):

- **Smoldering Scale:** Ember Beetle was 1.2% ×1 → **20% ×1**; Spark Bug was 0.8% ×1 → **12% ×1**. This is the Hot Edge start item; 1.2% made the quest feel broken (section 17).
- **Warm Grain:** Ember Beetle was 10% ×1 → **20% ×1–2**; Earth Sprite was 8% ×1 → **12% ×1–2**. Six grain should take a short hunt, not 60 kills.
- **Earth Shard:** Burrow Mole was 8% ×1–2 → **12% ×1–3**; Earth Sprite was 6% ×1–2 → **10% ×1–3**. Needed for Banks recipes at rank 2.
- **Tough Root:** Burrow Mole was 8% ×1–2 → **12% ×2–3**. Needed for rank 2–3 recipes in a level's worth of mole kills.
- **Fire Shard:** Ember Beetle was 5% ×1–2 → **10% ×1–3**. Flame Cleave's fuel.
- **Lightning Shard:** Spark Bug was 5% ×1–2 → **10% ×1–3**.
- **Stone Chunk:** Burrow Mole was 4% ×1–2 → **6% ×1–3**; Earth Sprite was 8% ×1–3 → **10% ×2–4**.
- **Ice Shard:** added on Spark Bug at **6% ×1–2** (section 13).
- Monster Essence, Nature Essence, Elemental Essence, Training Scroll, Clay, Twig, and the other materials: **same rates as v0.2.5**, higher stacks only.

**Acceptance checks (pacing and recipes)**

- With a fresh hunt in the Dirt Banks at level 4–5 (on the order of one level, ~38 moles or a mix of Banks regulars), the bag can afford **Power Strike rank 2** and, with a similar Nature Essence pile, **Earthbreaker rank 2**. The player does not need to return to Field Mice for Twigs.
- At level 7–8, after Banks hunting on the order of a level or two, **rank 3** of those skills is payable with Banks goods (Tough Root, Earth Shard, Stone Chunk, Earth Grit, Fire Shard, essences) in amounts of 10–40 — never a stack of 100+ Twigs or Pebbles.
- Nature production rank 1 costs Nature Essence 20 plus small pasture goods; Ila's new kit pays it exactly. Rank 2 costs Nature Essence 32 as the main gate, plus Clay and Earth Shard — no Pebble 80, no Bark 64.
- Earthbreaker rank 1 has **no Twig**. Rank 4 has **no Twig** and no material over 40.
- No live recipe asks for Heartwood or Log.
- Opening a loot pile from a Mole or Sprite shows clearly larger material and essence stacks than v0.2.5 (for example Twigs 2–5 instead of 1–3, Monster Essence 2–5 instead of 1–3 on moles).

## 16. Affinity: weaknesses and immunities

Combat skills have a **school**: Basic (no affinity), Nature, or Elemental. Affinity is the **school**, not the flavor of the animation.

| Attack | School |
| --- | --- |
| Basic (normal) attack | Basic |
| Power Strike | Basic |
| Rage (the damage bonus) | Basic — it multiplies whatever school the hit already is; it does not change school |
| Earthbreaker | **Nature** (even though the motion is a ground slam) |
| Root Lash | **Nature** |
| Flame Cleave | **Elemental** |
| Dig In | No damage; no affinity |
| Contact damage (section 11) | Basic |

**Multipliers**

| Relation | Damage | Player-facing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weak | **1.5×** after the normal damage is computed, before the hit is shown | A clear "Weak" (or similar) on the damage number |
| Normal | **1.0×** | No extra label |
| Immune | **0** — no HP is lost | A distinct **Immune** float; no damage number that looks like a soak |

There is no "resistant (50%)" band in this release.

**Per mob**

Nature / beast mobs are **weak to Elemental** (Flame Cleave hits 1.5×) and take normal damage from Nature and Basic. They are not immune to anything.

## 17. Hot Edge (Flame Cleave quest) — it must be startable

**What is wrong today.** Hot Edge starts only when a **Smoldering Scale** is picked up or inspected. That scale drops at **1.2%** from Ember Beetles (0.8% from Spark Bugs), so most players never see the quest. The journal does not tease item-started lines. Talking to Calden at level 6 with 10 STR does **not** start it. The quest feels broken.

Cinderback (the named beetle for the last step) must also actually appear, the same lesson as Bank-Cracker in v0.2.5.

**Expected behavior now**

1. **Calden can start it.** At **level 6**, Calden shows **`!`**. Talking starts Hot Edge, even if the player has no scale yet. He tells them to bring a Smoldering Scale from the Ember Beetles in the Dirt Banks. If they already have a scale and also have **10 STR**, this same talk can take the scale and advance to the Warm Grain step (see below). If they are level 6 but under 10 STR, the quest still **starts** (it is in the journal); he looks at the scale if they have it, says they are not ready, and **keeps the scale**. They come back at 10 STR for the `?`.
2. **The scale also starts it.** Picking up or inspecting a Smoldering Scale starts Hot Edge if it is not already started, at any level. A journal toast appears. The player can go to Calden. The **level 6 and 10 STR** gate applies to **turning the scale in**, not to discovering the quest. Before the gate, Calden's "not ready" lines play; the quest stays on the first step; the scale is not taken.
3. **The scale actually drops.** Ember Beetle **20% ×1**, Spark Bug **12% ×1** (section 15). In addition, the **first Ember Beetle killed at or after level 6** always drops a Smoldering Scale (once per character), so a player who is ready cannot miss it forever.
4. **Steps (unchanged in structure, clearer in start):**
   - Step 1: Bring the Smoldering Scale to Calden, at level 6 and 10 STR. He takes it (consumed) and asks for Warm Grain.
   - Step 2: Collect **6 Warm Grain** (Ember Beetles; Earth Sprites also drop some) and bring them to Calden. Consumed on turn-in. Progress shows as `Warm Grain n/6`.
   - Step 3: Defeat **Cinderback** in the Dirt Banks and bring its **Live Ember** to Calden. Completing **unlocks Flame Cleave training** (does **not** grant rank 1 for free — v0.2.3 rule, unchanged). Rewards: 140 XP, 25 Copper, Elemental Essence ×12, shown on the rewards screen.
5. **Cinderback spawn.** Cinderback is in the Dirt Banks from the moment step 2 is active through the hunt (steps 2 and 3), visibly distinct (larger, glowing shell), with a short toast when it appears ("Cinderback claws out of the bank," or similar). A kill during step 2 still counts. If it was missed, it is present again when the player returns, until it has been killed for this quest. Same spirit as Bank-Cracker from step 2 of A Crack in the Dirt.
6. Multi-line conversation stays (Calden's existing personality: gruff, patient, talks about heat and readiness). Enter or click advances; the quest only moves on the last page; Esc does not finish the step.

**Acceptance checks**

- A level 6 Warrior with 10 STR talks to Calden **without** ever having seen a scale: Hot Edge starts, Calden is marked, the journal has the quest, and he asks for a Smoldering Scale. This is the "cannot be stuck" path.
- A level 4 Warrior who picks up a Smoldering Scale gets the quest in the journal. Calden tells them they are not ready, does not take the scale, and no `?` shows. At level 6 with 10 STR, Calden shows `?` and the turn-in works.
- Ember Beetles drop Smoldering Scales often enough to see one in a short hunt; the first beetle at level 6 always drops one if the player has never received one.
- After the grain turn-in, Cinderback is visible in the Dirt Banks (not only on a hidden formal kill step), looks distinct from a regular beetle, and dropping it yields the Live Ember and lets the last turn-in complete.
- Completing the quest opens Flame Cleave **training**; the skill is not already at rank 1. The rewards screen lists 140 XP, 25 Copper, and 12 Elemental Essence.
- Inspecting a Smoldering Scale in the bag also starts the quest if it was not started.

## Out of scope / unchanged

- Warrior is the only playable class. Mage and Rogue, their skills, and their gear requirements stay unused.
- Soul Guard, Meadow Shrine, the east path ("That path is not open yet"), infusion, Spiritual, and Hunter's Luck stay parked. Do not open the Shrine to make Heartwood farmable in this release.
- Gear drop rates and magic-drop chance stay as in v0.2.5 (~1 in 150–200; about 1 in 12 of those is magic).
- Attack formula, skill rank damage scaling, hit chance, knockback, hit-stop, crit shake, sparks, and v0.2.5 skill cooldowns stay as they are.
- Player move speed (148), jump, gravity, melee reach (70), potions, food heals, MP, ability points per level (3), and death penalty stay as they are.
- Dig In is still a 60-second +50% defense buff. Rage still lasts as it does today and still only appears on C while active.
- Dialogue system (multi-page, Enter/click, Esc does not finish) stays. Only Ila's kit lines need to name the new items. Calden's Hot Edge lines follow section 17.
- Quest requirements (counts, givers, order, and who grants a skill vs. who opens training) stay, except Hot Edge's **start** is fixed as in section 17. Bank-Cracker's v0.2.5 spawn rules stay.
- Existing character saves are not wiped. Old kit items already in a bag stay. New recipe costs apply when the player next trains or upgrades. Shop items already in a bag keep the stats they rolled; new purchases are exact.
- Parked skill and passive cost rows (Soul Guard, Mage, Rogue, Spiritual, Hunter's Luck) may be left as they currently are until those features go live; then they should follow the same recipe rules.
- Animation frame rates for mobs (Crow 1, others 0.5) and the hero walk cycle are unchanged. Only mob **travel** speed changes.
- No new class, no new area, no new live skill, no new live passive node.

## Open questions for Oded

Please confirm these two calls; everything else in this document is a decided design.

1. **HP and contact together.** Warrior HP is 100 base and +7 per level (was 120 and +10), and contact damage is 40% of a normal hit. Is that the right toughness, or should HP come down less (for example 110 and +8) now that contact exists?
2. **Late ranks while the east is closed.** Ranks 3–10 of live skills and passives use only Dirt Banks materials, with Earth Crystal at 5 and 10, and never Heartwood, Log, or Meadow Boar goods. That is so those ranks are actually payable before the Shrine and Stump Hollow open. When those areas open, later ranks can be revisited. Is that the right temporary shape?
