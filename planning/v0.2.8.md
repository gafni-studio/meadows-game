# Meadows v0.2.8 — Magician Path

The second Path, built on the tree UI and Path rules shipped in v0.2.7. Keep it **minimal**: one new path, a tutorial, and a polished feel for its skills. No new area in this release. Design only; the developer has room to interpret.

Path rules are unchanged from v0.2.7: any Hero can train any path; a skill needs the previous skill in its chain plus materials; no level gate; pacing guide green ~ level 5, blue ~ level 10, orange ~ level 20 (orange still out of scope); the family’s essence is the main cost; going deep should beat splitting. Recipe rules stay as in v0.2.6: amounts about 10–40, materials from mobs near that rank’s pace, essence as the main gate.

---

## The Magician Path

A **ranged, MP-hungry** path. The Hero fights from a distance and spends more MP than the Warrior Path for the same stretch of combat. This is a feel target: Magic Bolt and the later attacks are projectiles; Mana Shield is the defensive basic; MP potions and regen matter more than they do for a Warrior-Path Hero.

**Skills, in chain order.** Use these names (the first two are renames of the current unused mage basics).

```
Magic Bolt (basic, root)          — rename of Energy Blast
  └── Mana Shield (basic)         — rename of Energy Shield
        ├── Fire Bolt (Elemental)
        └── Thorn Volley (Nature)
              └── Chain Spark (Elemental)
```

- **Magic Bolt** — the root. A readable bolt of raw energy thrown from a distance. The first Magician attack.
- **Mana Shield** — the basic defensive follow-up: a ward that dulls incoming hits. Same role as today’s Energy Shield, under the new name.
- **Fire Bolt** — a searing Elemental bolt that hits harder than Magic Bolt and leaves a burn. (Renamed from the unused Firebolt if that helps; player-facing name is Fire Bolt.)
- **Thorn Volley** — a Nature volley of thorns, a different shape from Fire Bolt (a spread or a short burst of projectiles, not a single bolt).
- **Chain Spark** — an Elemental spark that jumps from foe to foe. The deepest skill in this release, paced toward blue.

**Not in this release (future content):** Frost Shard (something else will replace that slot later), Spirit Drain, and any other Spirit skills. Leave a note or an empty slot if it helps planning; do not implement them.

### Recipes

Use **existing Meadow and Dirt Banks materials** only — Twig, Bark, Wool, Fur, Pebble, Clay, Earth Grit, Earth Shard, Fire Shard, Lightning Shard, Nature Essence, Elemental Essence, Monster Essence, Training Scroll, Copper, and the other goods already on those mobs. No new items and no Whisperwood materials.

Examples, not a full table:

- Magic Bolt rank 1 (green, Pasture pace): Monster Essence and Training Scrolls in the 10–40 band, similar in spirit to Power Strike’s early recipe, perhaps a little more expensive because the path comes later.
- Fire Bolt rank 1 (green-to-blue, Banks): Elemental Essence as the main cost, plus Earth Grit and Fire Shard.
- Thorn Volley rank 1: Nature Essence as the main cost, plus Pasture-to-Banks Nature goods (for example Dry Root or Bark in modest amounts).
- Chain Spark, later ranks: Elemental Essence toward the top of the 10–40 band, plus Lightning Shard and other Banks elemental goods.

Same “deep path beats spreading” idea as the Warrior Path.

## Look and feel of new skills

New Magician skills must feel **nice to use**, not like placeholders.

- **Readable projectiles:** the player can see the bolt, volley or spark in the air and tell which skill it is.
- **Impact effects** at the moment damage lands, timed to the projectile arriving, not to the key press.
- **Family-colored VFX:** basic energy reads as a neutral spark; Fire Bolt is clearly fire; Thorn Volley is clearly nature; Chain Spark is clearly lightning.
- A **satisfying hit feel** (a crisp impact, a short hit-stop or snap, sparks) in the same spirit as a good Warrior Path connect.

Mana Shield should have a clear, brief ward appearance when it is put on, and a readable look while it lasts — then it ends cleanly with the buff.

## Magician Path tutorial

A **short tutorial quest from a new town NPC** (a Magician-minded character in Meadow Town — give them a short personality; they are not Calden, Mara, Pell or Ila). It introduces this Path the way Calden’s v0.2.7 tutorial introduced the Warrior Path:

- The Magician Path is a second Path a Hero can walk; it is ranged and spends more MP.
- The chain starts at Magic Bolt, then Mana Shield, then Fire Bolt and Thorn Volley, then Chain Spark.
- Families and essence still apply (Elemental Essence for Fire Bolt and Chain Spark, Nature Essence for Thorn Volley).
- Open the tree, switch to the Magician tab, train the root, equip it in an attack slot.

The new NPC is the giver; town layout can place them in Meadow Town without a new area. Keep it a short chain. Completing it should leave the player with Magic Bolt trained (or ready to train) and a reason to care about MP.
