# v0.2.5 — Attack feel, drops, quests, and conversation

Playtest notes on v0.2.4. Warrior only. Soul Guard, the Meadow Shrine, and infusion stay parked. Gear drop rates stay very rare (~1 in 150–200); the new quest gifts are fixed mid rolls, not drop-table changes. Short player-facing `desc`s; design in `notes`.

## In plain words, per note

1. **Shop sell price.** On Mara’s **Sell** tab the preview shows “Sells for Xc” (what she pays). The inventory tooltip still hides it. The Buy tab shows shop price, not sell price.
2. **Attack feel (top priority).** A basic swing now draws a proper slash trail that reaches the real melee range (70 px). Damage lands on the impact frame, when the blade crosses the hitting line — not at the start of the press. A few frames of hit-stop freeze the world on a connect; a crit adds a small screen shake and gold sparks. Power Strike is a heavy overhead smash with an impact flash. Earthbreaker cracks the ground and sends a shockwave along its range. Flame Cleave is a fire arc. Root Lash erupts roots from the dirt. Rage bursts a red aura on cast. All drawn with particles, tweens and graphics.
3. **Drop rates.** Exact numbers as requested. All four named items already existed (`strawberry`, `blueberry`, `stone_chunk`, `monster_essence`, `nature_essence`, `training_scroll`, `tough_root`, `branch`). Nothing new to invent. Monster Essence stays more common than category essence on every mob we touched. Earth Shard was also added on Burrow Mole (8% ×1–2) so the Earthbreaker recipe is farmable in the Banks.
4. **Earthbreaker recipe.** Rank 1: **Earth Shard ×3** replaces Branch ×3, **Clay ×30** replaces Bark ×30. Clay already drops commonly from Burrow Moles (36%). Earth Shard already drops from Earth Sprites (6% ×1–2) and now also from moles.
5. **Dig In.** A simple buff: **60 seconds, +50% defense**. The old 0.5s timed block, knockback immunity, and “dug in” shield behaviour are gone. The stuck-forever aura was a clock bug: the glow compared a wall-clock expiry to the scene clock, so once turned on it never went out. The earth aura now uses `Date.now()`, and it ends with the buff. Player desc: “Set your feet. For a minute, blows land softer.”
6. **Rage on the character sheet.** The real cause was not the sprite tint (that already expired with the buff). The character window (`C`) drew a Rage row whenever the skill was *known*, with the words “when active.” It now only appears while the Rage buff is actually on. Verified by a screenshot of the window with Rage known and off.
7. **Bank-Cracker.** It only spawned on the formal kill-step (step 4), after the pebble turn-in, and it was tinted almost the same brown as a regular mole — easy to miss. It now appears from the moment you enter the Banks on A Crack in the Dirt (step 2 onward), with a pale stone-grey tint and a larger body, and a toast: “Bank-Cracker claws out of the bank.” A kill during step 2 or 3 still counts. Verified through the Earthbreaker quest flow in smoke.
8. **Attack speed ~17% faster.** Hero basic interval is `0.83 / (1 + AGI × 0.005)` seconds (was 1.0s). Mobs recover and hesitate ~17% sooner (`recoverMs` [125, 375], `approachDelayMs` [0, 415]); telegraphs are only about 10% shorter so they stay readable.
9. **Attacking-skill cooldowns ~20% shorter.** Power Strike 0.8s (was 1.0), Earthbreaker 2.4s (was 3.0), Flame Cleave 2.0s (was 2.5), Root Lash 1.6s (was 2.0). Rage (60s), Dig In (4s) and parked Soul Guard (30s) are unchanged.
10. **Mob attack range.** Ember Beetle 22 px (pincers), Meadow Boar 28 px (lunge), Earth Sprite 24 px, Spark Bug 18 px (snap), Bank-Cracker 20 px (heavy arms). Everyone else stays at contact (4 px).
11. **Two resource-and-gear quests.** **A Warm Coat** (Mara, after The Quiet Work): 8 Wool and 6 Fur from the pasture, reward 90 XP, 20 copper, a mid-roll **Hide Vest**, and 4 Blueberries. **What the Dirt Keeps** (Pell, after The Dirt Path): 8 Earth Grit and 4 Stone Chunk from the Banks, reward 140 XP, 30 copper, a mid-roll **Stone Club** (STR 5), and Nature Essence ×6. Quest gifts do not change drop tables.
12. **The Quiet Work** (Ila). Starts when The Weight of a Swing is done. Talk to Ila, bring 8 Twig and 4 Bark; she gives a kit equal to the Nature Essence rank-1 cost (pebble 50, bark 40, earth_shard 5, nature_essence 50, copper 30) and tells you to open T and start a first rank. Return when you have. Rewards: 80 XP, 15 copper, Meadow Dew ×2. Spending that first point is still The Dirt Path’s prereq — she points you at Pell. Existing saves that already have a rank just talk through and finish.
13. **Conversation.** Quest start, progress and turn-in at Calden, Mara, Pell and Ila are multi-line. One short thought per page, Enter or click to advance, Esc closes without finishing the step. They have personality: Calden is gruff and patient; Mara is practical and wry; Pell is warm and knows the paths; Ila talks in growing-things. Pell’s default greeting is a few lore lines about the Meadows and the Banks.

## Judgment calls

- Item names mapped to existing ids: Stone Chunk → `stone_chunk`, Branch → `branch`, Earth Shard → `earth_shard`, Clay → `clay`. No new items.
- Earth Shard on Burrow Mole (8% ×1–2) so three shards for Earthbreaker aren’t only a Sprite farm.
- Bank-Cracker is visible from Crack step 2, not only step 4, because “it doesn’t show up” was the actual complaint; the kill still counts early.
- Bank-Cracker’s old tint `0xc4a070` was too close to a mole. It’s pale stone-grey and larger now.
- Ila’s kit equals the exact rank-1 Nature Essence cost so “guide spending the first point” is possible at this level. It’s given mid-quest (step 2), not as the final reward.
- Quest gear is a **mid, non-magic** roll (`makeGear(..., { mode: 'mid', magic: false })`), a gift, not a drop — the 150–200 band is unchanged.
- Warm Coat waits until The Quiet Work is done so Mara’s shop still opens on E at the start of the game.
- Hit-stop is ~3 frames on a basic connect, ~4 on a crit, longer on Power Strike. Physics and tweens pause; scene timers still run so the next impact callback can fire.
- Cooldown cuts apply only to attacking skills, 20%.
- Mob range picks are the ones that already “want” a lunge or a longer swipe. Crow stays a ranged pebble thrower.
- Dialogue advances the quest on the **last** OK, not the first page, so you can Esc and come back. Smoke dismisses every page with Enter.

## Where the authoritative source lives

Oded’s Google Sheet is outdated; do **not** auto-update it from here. A full sync should come from the **repo**:

- **Source of truth for tabulated design:** `sheet/Game_Design_DB_v0.xlsx`, exported by `npm run export-sheet` into `data/*.json` (items, drop_tables, skills, skill_costs, skill_quests, npcs, monsters, gear, combat_formulas, map_layout, and the rest). This v0.2.5 patch is `scripts/sheet-patches/v0.2.5.py`. After export, `data/` matches the xlsx.
- **Source of truth for live combat numbers that the sheet historically left as prose:** `src/data/assumptions.ts` — skill cooldowns (`ASSUME.cooldownMs`), mob speeds, `AI` recover/hesitation, `MONSTER_FEEL` range and windup, melee reach, knockback, ANIM. v0.2.5 also records those live values on **combat_formulas** (`attack_interval`, `skill_cooldown_attacking`, `mob_attack_speed`, `mob_attack_range`) so a sheet sync has them. The running game reads Player.attackIntervalMs and ASSUME, not those extra formula rows.
- **Conversation text** lives in `src/systems/Quests.ts` (`TALK`), not in a sheet column. Short player `desc`s stay on the quest rows.
- Attack formula is still `floor(weapon × mult) + floor(mainStat / 2)` (v0.2.3, hidden). `combat_formulas.damage_formula` notes were updated to say so (they still mentioned the old +1%/stat).
