# v0.3.0 — The Split Meadows and Whisperwood

The first large content release, from `planning/v0.3.0.md`. Data changes are in `data/*.json` (no sheet patch); new art comes from `scripts/draw-art-v030.py`.

## In plain words, per section

1. **The map is split, with vertical levels.** Town → **Meadow West** → **Meadow East** → **Dirt Banks West** → **Dirt Banks East**, a portal at both ends of each half. The world now scrolls up as well as sideways.
   - **Climbing:** stand at a ladder, rope, vine or root ladder and press **Up / W** to climb, **Down / S** to go down (also from the top of one). **Space** lets go; with a direction held you jump off sideways. Up still jumps when there is nothing to climb.
   - **Dropping:** **Down + Space** on a one-way ledge drops through it. Solid ground (hills, banks, the cave floor) can't be dropped through. Half-tile slopes are walked up without jumping.
   - **Meadow West** (1–3): flat, a fence, one low ledge, no climbing. Field Mouse 8, Wool Sheep 6.
   - **Meadow East** (3–7, grove 10): rolling hills with Crows (4) on the ground and Wool-Shoulder on the far ground; three terraces with **Meadow Boars** (4) up short ladders from the hilltops; a raised **grove** up a rope with the **Old Stump** and, behind it, the road north to the Whisperwood.
   - **Dirt Banks West** (4–5): a river along the bottom, mud shelves (stepping stones up), high bank ledges on root ladders. Burrow Mole 6, Earth Sprite 5.
   - **Dirt Banks East** (4–6): a cave. The cave floor, three upper galleries on ropes (Cinderback on the east one), and a lower pit you drop into: Bank-Cracker's den, ropes back up.
   - Mobs patrol their own level and never walk off it. An aggressive mob that can't reach you gives up as before; ledge mobs give up as soon as you leave their level (a Thornling still throws at a climber one tier up).
   - Quest text and dialogue now name the halves (mice and wool in Meadow West, crows and Wool-Shoulder in Meadow East, grit and stone in Dirt Banks West, beetles, Bank-Cracker and Cinderback in the Dirt Banks East cave). Pell's lore mentions the hills, the river and the cave.
2. **Whisperwood** (5–10), north of Meadow East's grove. Before Mist on the North Road reaches its second step, the grove portal says *"The mist is too thick to follow. Pell might know the way."*
   - **Wren's camp** at the forest edge (tent, campfire): Wren, the woodwarden, no shop. Mobs never aggro or chase into the camp.
   - **Forest floor** (a mossy bank): Wisps near the entrance, Thornlings further in; it steps down into the **bog** with Mire Toads; past the bog, the **Hollow**, a ring of dead trees with the **Hollow Stag**.
   - **Canopy**: three tiers of branch ledges with jumpable gaps, vines and ropes between them. Moss Lurkers and three more Wisps.
   - **Mobs** use the spec's level, HP, attack, defense, XP and family. Wisp: passive, floats and bobs, throws slow glowing motes once hit and keeps its distance. Thornling: aggressive, hops in jittery bursts, throws green thorns, scratches when you're close. Moss Lurker: a still, breathing mound until you come within 3 tiles (or hit it), then pops up and lunge-bites after a short pause; slow and hard to push. Mire Toad: passive, big hops that land with a splash, puffs its cheeks, then a tongue lash (~44 px).
   - **Affinity:** the four nature mobs and the Stag take 1.5× from Flame Cleave, Fire Bolt and Chain Spark; the Wisp takes 1.5× from Earthbreaker, Root Lash and Thorn Volley; nothing is immune.
   - **The Hollow Stag** (level 10, 1,600 HP, 3 min respawn) never leaves the Hollow. Charge (head down, paws the ground, then rushes the clearing), antler sweep (sways back, then a wide arc), root stomp (rears tall, stomps; root spikes erupt outward along the ground, so jump or stand on something). At half HP the moss-light in its ribs brightens, the mist thickens, stomps come more often, a charge chains straight into a sweep and it gets a little faster. Every big hit has a shockwave, screen shake and dust. It kneels and dissolves into mist and fireflies as the clearing brightens. Leave the Hollow and it walks home and heals.
   - **Materials:** Wisp Dust, Moss Fiber, Toad Gland and the Hollow Antler trophy, with the spec's drop tables. Ila takes Moss Fiber and Toad Gland (→ Nature Essence) and Wisp Dust (→ Spirit Essence) at 10:1; Mara doesn't buy them.
3. **The Whisperwood chain**, exact rewards and copy from the spec:
   - **Mist on the North Road** (Pell, level 5 after Beyond the Pasture Gate): talk to Pell, follow the road into the Whisperwood, find Wren. 30 XP, 6 Copper.
   - **Lanterns That Wander** (Wren, after it): 6 Wisps and 4 Wisp Dust. 40 XP, 8 Copper.
   - **What Waits in the Branches** (Wren, level 7): 5 Moss Lurkers, 3 Thornlings, 4 Moss Fiber. 50 XP, 10 Copper, 1 Meadow Dew.
   - **The Drowned Hollow** (Wren, level 8): gather 3 Toad Gland, follow the tracks to the edge of the Hollow, return. 55 XP, 10 Copper.
   - **Antlers in the Mist** (Wren, level 9): defeat the Hollow Stag and bring its antler. 80 XP, 15 Copper, 3 Nature Essence. The first Stag kill during the quest always gives the antler.
   - Quest items are consumed at the turn-in; no dialogue gives materials; every completion plays the banner, then the rewards screen.
4. **Pacing:** no mob above level 10, no orange content; the blue fights are the Stag and the Old Stump.
5. **Look and feel:** every new mob has its own sprite sheet (idle/move frames and a wind-up pose; the Lurker also a mound), its own telegraph, hit reaction and death (Wisp sprays light, Thornling bursts into leaves, Lurker slumps into moss, Toad belly-flops with a splash). Whisperwood has layered fog in parallax, light shafts, fireflies, swaying vines and bog ripples; Meadow East has grass tufts leaning in the wind; Dirt Banks West has glints moving along the river; Dirt Banks East has a rock ceiling, drips, wall glints and torchlight around the pit.
6. **Rank recipes follow the map.** Every rank of the 11 Path skills uses the spec's new materials, exactly as tabled; essence, Training Scroll and Copper are unchanged. Hollow Antler at rank 10 of Earthbreaker, Flame Cleave, Fire Bolt and Chain Spark. Ranks already learned are untouched.

## Judgment calls

- **Old Stump "daytime":** there is no day/night cycle yet, so it is always there (its night twin stays parked).
- **The cave pit** is the base ground of Dirt Banks East; the cave floor is solid rock four tiles up. Plank ledges stick out over the pit where the ropes reach the top.
- **Mud shelves** are reached by short stepping stones (jumps), the high banks by root ladders; the canopy by vines from the floor and ropes between tiers.
- **Mist on the North Road** has three steps (talk to Pell, reach the Whisperwood, talk to Wren), so Pell can say his "not yet" line on the way. The road opens at step 2.
- **The Drowned Hollow** has four steps: glands (moves on by itself once you have 3), reach the edge of the Hollow (x ≥ 146, outside the Stag's ring), return to Wren with the glands.
- **An NPC with several quests** now leads with the one that moves forward. Before, What the Banks Hide's "not yet" would have hidden Mist on the North Road behind it at Pell.
- **Wren's sprite** is Pell's in woodwarden green (placeholder, like Tamsin's).
- **Moss Lurker range** 30, **Mire Toad** 44, per the spec; their attack speed and the Stag's timings (charge wind-up 0.9 s, sweep 0.65 s, stomp 0.8 s; stomp every 9 s, 5.2 s in phase 2) are first guesses for playtesting.
- **Saves** in Sunny Pasture land in Meadow West, in the Dirt Banks in Dirt Banks West.
