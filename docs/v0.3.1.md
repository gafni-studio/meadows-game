# v0.3.1 — Playtest fixes: west of town

A fixes release from `planning/v0.3.1.md`. Data changes are in `data/*.json`; new art comes from `scripts/draw-art-v031.py`.

## In plain words, per section

1. **A west side for the town.** Left to right: **Sunny Pasture → Meadow West → Meadow Town → Meadow East → Dirt Banks West → Dirt Banks East**; Whisperwood stays north of Meadow East's grove.
   - **Sunny Pasture** (3–5, new, far west): a wide, gently rolling meadow with two low ledges and no climbing, fences, bushes and a lone big tree. A dead end. Wool Sheep 6, Crow 5, Boar 2 (the Boars at the far end).
   - **Meadow West** (1–3) moved west of town and is mirrored: the town gate is on its east edge. Mice by the gate (8), sheep in the middle (6), two Crows at the far-left end. Its west portal leads to Sunny Pasture.
   - **Meadow Town** has a west gate; Pell stands by it. The east gate now leads straight to Meadow East.
   - **Meadow East**: Crows 4 and Wool Sheep 4 on the ground (Wool-Shoulder still on the far ground), Boars 4 on the terraces, one Angry Bull in the raised grove.
2. **Angry Bull, the Boar, no Stump.** The Meadow Boar is renamed the **Angry Bull** (same level 7, 225 HP, 47 XP, same drops with the new ranges). It is drawn darker and redder, a bit bigger, and snorts and paws the ground before its charge. The **Old Stump** no longer spawns. The new **Boar** (level 5, 140 HP, 27 attack, 8 defense, 30 XP, aggressive) is a small bristly side-view boar: a head-down wind-up, a short charge with a dust puff, a squeal when hit, and it tips over on death. Drops as specified.
3. **West first.** All the copy in the spec is in: Welcome to Meadow Town, Path of the Warrior, Beyond the Pasture Gate, The Cracked Stone, every "Pell at the west gate" objective, Pell's chat lines and his NPC description.
4. **Mara's slap.** After the strawberry purchase Mara slaps the Hero for 18 HP (a hand swipe, a red flash, the number, a small knockback; never below 1 HP). Talking to her again gives her two lines, the Hero eats the berry, and a third talk gives "There. Feel better? Good. Go see Ila…" and the Meadow Dew. The chest steps are gone (the chain is ten steps now); the chest still works.
5. **Skill tree and objectives.**
   - After Path of the Warrior / Magician, closing the rewards screen opens the Paths window on that tab with Power Strike / Magic Bolt and its rank-1 cost pulsing. The pulse ends on the first click in the window or when it closes; once per Path.
   - Objectives (tracker and journal) are one general line with count lines underneath (`Twig: 0/5`, `Field Mouse: 0/5`), each ticked when complete. Objectives that used to carry numbers were reworded to general lines.
   - **Farm list:** the skill preview has **Add to farm list**; the entry appears in green under the quests in the objectives panel as *Farm: Power Strike rank 3* with the recipe as count lines. Click again or ✕ to remove; it disappears once that rank is trained.
   - **Ready to train:** a node whose next rank is fully affordable glows in its family colour with a ▲.
   - A quest ready to turn in shows its title bold gold with a ✓ and brighter count lines.
6. **Plain stat wording.** The stat-point descriptions now read "Raises attack and max HP.", "Raises max MP and magic damage.", "Raises speed and crit.", "Raises HP regen and MP regen." Gear and passive descriptions already had no numbers.
7. **Combat fixes.** Hit invulnerability is 1.0 s (was 0.7 s). The crow bug: ground spawn zones reported height 0, so every ground mob spawned at the base ground even on a hill. Ground mobs now spawn on the solid ground under their own x.
8. **Drop ranges.** Level 1–3 mobs: 2–3 → 1–2 (and Crow's Tough Root 1–3 → 1–2, so no Mouse/Sheep/Crow stack of 3 except Feather). Level 4+: 2–3 → 1–3. Mouse and Sheep scrolls 9% → 12%. Crow Feather 40%, 1–3.

## Judgment calls

- Internal id `meadow_boar` became `angry_bull` (art file renamed); the new mob is `boar`. Old saves are unaffected (mobs are not saved).
- Mara's first line ("That's for walking in…") shows as a toast with the slap, and both her lines play again when you talk to her, so the step is still a conversation.
- Old saves partway through the intro move past the removed chest steps (step 7 → 6, 8+ → one less). The old `sunny_pasture` save alias is gone because Sunny Pasture is a real area again.
- Boar AI is the standard melee AI with a stronger lunge (`MOB_KIND.boar.lungeVx`) and a longer wind-up; the Angry Bull's wind-up is longer for its snort and paw. Boar speed 40, range 34 px. These are first guesses for playtesting.
- Hud.ts was split: the Paths window (`ui/panels/skills.ts`) and journal (`ui/panels/journal.ts`) moved out (pure move, separate commit); the tracker is `ui/panels/tracker.ts`, the farm list `systems/Farm.ts`, the slap visual `scenes/world/slap.ts`.
