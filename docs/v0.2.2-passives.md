# v0.2.2 — Nature + Elemental passive tree, Ila's 10:1 bench

The first playable slice of the passive trees. Spiritual and Hunter's Luck stay parked.

## Production

Each tree has one production node that yields **only** that tree's category essence:

- Rate = `1.5 × 1.6^(rank−1)` essence/hour at rank ≥ 1 (~1.5/h at rank 1, ~9.8 at r5, ~103 at r10). Rank 0 yields nothing.
- Accrues while you play and while you are away, from timestamps. Fractional progress is kept; whole essence is added to the inventory the next time the game settles (on load, every few seconds, or when you open the tree).
- Upgrade time is `10 × 1.6^(targetRank−1)` minutes, paid up front, one upgrade at a time. The current rank keeps producing until the timer finishes (including offline). A mid-offline completion splits the interval at the finish time, then uses the new rate.

Open the tree with **T**. Nature and Elemental are tabs (Tab to switch). Keyboard-first tiles, same as skills.

## Efficiency

- **Forager's Eye** (Nature) and **Elemental Attunement**: +2% category material and category-essence drop rate per rank while hunting. Does not apply to Monster Essence or Neutral junk.

## Ila's bench

Ila in Meadow Town opens a 10:1 exchange (data on the `exchange` tab). Twig, Bark, Pebble, Clay, Wool, Feather, Fur, and Dry Root → Nature Essence. Earth Grit and the listed shards → Elemental Essence. Holy Dust and Soul Dust → Spirit Essence. Enter exchanges one batch.

## Costs

Rank-1 rows live on `passive_costs`. Higher ranks scale `round(qty × 1.6^(r−1))`, except a rank-r row that names a rank-1 material **overrides** the scaled qty (the sheet already lists r2 and r3 in full). Rares at r5 and r10 are additional (stone chunk / heartwood on Nature; earth crystal / holy essence on Elemental).
