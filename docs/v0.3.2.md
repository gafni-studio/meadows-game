# v0.3.2: Meadow East from the tile kit (look test)

A visual-only test of the new kit in the real game. No spec; the brief was "build Meadow East from the kit so Oded can judge the look".

## What changed
- **Meadow East only** (`geo.kit = 'meadow_east'`). Layout, collision, spawns, climbing: untouched. The old collision bodies stay, just invisible.
- **Backdrop:** painted sky (fixed), far hills (scroll 0.2), near hills (scroll 0.45), tiled sideways with alternate copies mirrored.
- **Ground and hills:** `ground_cap_left`, long `ground_top`, `ground_cap_right` with `ground_fill` flush below; the three hill mounds are stacked runs, taller ones over lower ones.
- **Terraces:** `platform_left/mid/right` with `platform_under_*` tucked 16 kit px under the body.
- **Ladders and ropes:** top, mid (tiled), bottom pieces. Rails hook over the ledge lip; feet sit in the ground grass. Rope loop sits on the lip.
- **Portals:** the kit still, glow base in the grass.
- **Front grass:** `grass_front` at the same x and phase as the top tiles, drawn above player, mobs and NPCs (depth 10.5), below health bars and text. Stretches hidden behind a taller hill are skipped so the tips never float over dirt.
- **No gray shadow ellipses:** the game never drew any under characters; none were added. The old hill-wind grass tufts are off on this map (the kit has its own grass).

## Judgment calls
- **Scale:** the kit is drawn for 1920x1080 with a 160 px hero; the game runs at 960x540 with a 48 px hero. Kit tiles are scaled to 5/16 (whole pixels for every kit size) by `scripts/build-east-kit.py` into `public/art/east/`. Feet sit 5 px below the walking line (kit: 16-20 px).
- **Old decor hidden** (trees, bushes, fences, signs are placeholder art and clash); tell me if the signs should stay.
- The `oneway_*` pieces are unused: every Meadow East ledge is 16+ tiles wide and reads like the proof's thick terrace, so `platform_*` + undersides fit.
