# v0.2.6 — Rewards screen, markers, tougher mobs, real recipes

Playtest notes on v0.2.5, implemented from `planning/v0.2.6.md`. Warrior only. Soul Guard, the Meadow Shrine, infusion, Spiritual and Hunter's Luck stay parked. Gear drop rates are unchanged. The sheet patch is `scripts/sheet-patches/v0.2.6.py`; it reads the recipe and drop tables straight out of the spec, so the numbers match it exactly.

## In plain words, per section

1. **Quest rewards screen.** Finishing a quest now opens a **Rewards** screen after the last conversation page: the quest name and "Complete", one flavor line, then XP, Copper, each item (`1× Hide Vest — Defense 3`) and the skill line (`Power Strike rank 1`, or `Flame Cleave training is now open`). Enter or a click on **Continue** closes it. It replaces the old QUEST COMPLETE banner. Quest gift gear is the exact typical piece and never magic (Hide Vest Defense 3, Stone Club weapon damage 16).
2. **Attack animations.** The normal attack is now the old Power Strike overhead smash, landing at the end of the 70 px range with dust. Power Strike is a new forward axe cut from the rear hip with a follow-through and a dust-and-spark burst. Flame Cleave is a wide fire fan to the end of its range with embers, a scorch on the ground and a fire bloom on each target; damage lands when the fire front reaches each target. Earthbreaker, Root Lash, Rage and Dig In are untouched. Damage now fires from inside the animation (the smash landing, the blade going level), so the visual and the hit cannot drift apart.
3. **Quest markers.** Calden, Mara, Pell and Ila show a gold bobbing `!` (a quest is available or its opening talk is waiting) or `?` (a turn-in is ready). `?` wins. Nothing while a step is in progress or a gate is unmet. Markers hide while any window is open and update the moment an objective completes.
4. **Objectives from the journal.** J has an **Objectives (L)** button that shows the tracker. L still toggles it. The tracker moved under the status box so it never sits behind a window.
5. **Player HP.** Warrior 100 base, +7 per level (was 120 and +10). STR is still +2. Mage 70 + 4, Rogue 85 + 6 (parked, recorded in the sheet).
6. **Exact shop gear + gear on C.** Mara's gear is fixed and never magic: cloth set Defense 6 (1, 2, 1, 1, 1), Short Sword weapon damage 13 × 1.10. The Character screen lists each equipped piece with its Defense / weapon damage and every affix, a "from gear" totals line, and total stat numbers with `(+N from gear)`.
7. **Passive production is whole numbers.** Ranks 1–10 make 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 18, 28, 42, 64 per hour (Nature and Elemental, online and offline).
8. **Mob speed and reach.** Travel speed is up about 45–50% and every mob has a longer reach (Boar 44 is the longest of the regulars). Animation frame rates are unchanged.
9. **Mob AI.** Idle mobs patrol their whole spawn stretch. An aggroed mob that cannot reach the player for ~2.75 s drops aggro and patrols again; it re-aggros when the player is reachable. Mobs can now stand on platforms and jump up to a ledge up to 80 px high (a low ledge, not a 4-tile one).
10. **Progress counts.** Collect and hunt steps show `Twig 3/8 · Bark 1/4` (capped at the requirement) on L and in the journal, live.
11. **Contact damage.** A mob in combat bumps for 40% of a swing when its body touches the player, physical, with a small nudge and the usual ~0.7 s invulnerability. An unaggroed passive mob does nothing.
12. **No skill queue.** A press on cooldown is ignored and silent. Only Rage (60 s) shows one "on cooldown" banner per cooldown; nothing fires when a cooldown ends.
13. **Frost Moth leaves the Banks.** Banks are now mole 6, sprite 5, beetle 5, spark bug 4 (20 regulars). Spark Bug drops Ice Shard (6% × 1–2).
14. **Junk.** Broken Twig, Torn Cloth, Lost Button, Pasture Bell and Cracked Shell no longer drop (existing ones can still be sold).
15. **XP and recipes.** From level 4, a level takes twice the XP (4→5 is 880, 10→11 is 3130). Every live skill and passive has an exact recipe at every rank 1–10, amounts 10–40, no Heartwood or Log, Earth Crystal at ranks 5 and 10. Ila's kit now equals Nature rank 1 exactly (Nature Essence 20, Pebble 16, Bark 12, Copper 18). Resource stacks are about 1.5–2× larger, with the named rate bumps from the spec.
16. **Affinity.** Flame Cleave is Elemental: Weak (×1.5) on beasts, **Immune** on Earth Sprite, Ember Beetle, Spark Bug and Cinderback. Earthbreaker and Root Lash are Nature: Weak on the elemental mobs. Basic attacks, Power Strike and contact are never weak or immune.
17. **Hot Edge is startable.** At level 6 Calden shows `!`; talking starts it even with no scale. A scale picked up or inspected at any level starts it too; the level 6 / 10 STR gate applies to turning the scale in (Calden keeps his hands off it until then). Scales drop 20% (beetle) / 12% (spark bug), and the first Ember Beetle killed at level 6+ always drops one. Cinderback is out in the Banks from the grain step, with a toast.

## Judgment calls

- The task said "v.0.0.26"; I implemented `planning/v0.2.6.md` (the only open spec, the repo was at v0.2.5).
- **Contact damage has no hit roll** (a bump is not a swing); it uses the same attack roll, ×0.4, then defense and the minimum-damage rule. "In combat" means any state other than idle, returning or dead.
- **Leash origin.** With a patrol that spans a whole spawn stretch, the 20-tile leash is measured from where the mob was when it started the fight, not from its spawn point, and it returns there.
- **Mobs on platforms.** Mobs now collide with the same one-way ledges as the player. A mob that dies on a ledge respawns on the ground.
- **Named foes drop their base mob's table** (Wool-Shoulder → Wool Sheep, Bank-Cracker → Burrow Mole, Cinderback → Ember Beetle). They had none before.
- **Spec names vs the sheet.** The spec's "Linen Cap/Vest/Pants/Boots/Gloves" are the sheet's Hood/Robe/Wraps/Sandals/Sleeves; I kept the sheet names.
- **Shop purchases** were going through the random drop roll (could come out magic). They now use the sheet's exact values and are never magic.
- **Immune hits** still wake a passive mob but do not push it; no burn is applied.
- **A skill pressed while the basic swing is still recovering** is "not ready", so it is ignored rather than queued (basic-attack buffering is unchanged).
- **The Weight of a Swing** completes on the third post hit with no conversation, so its rewards screen appears at that moment.
- **Shrine Wisp** lost its Lost Button row (the only junk in a table the spec did not list in full).
- **Power Strike's reach** is the same 70 px as the basic attack.
- The basic smash keeps the old Power Strike sword motion exactly as it was in v0.2.5.
