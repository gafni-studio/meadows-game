# v0.2.9 — Fixes before Whisperwood

A fixes-and-feel release from `planning/v0.2.9.md`. No new area, skills, items or mobs. It reworks how a new Hero is welcomed and paced. Sheet patch: `scripts/sheet-patches/v0.2.9.py`.

## In plain words, per section

1. **Path trees open only when earned.** K shows no tree for a new Hero, only *"Calden in Meadow Town can set you on a Path."* Finishing **Path of the Warrior** reveals the Warrior tab, **Path of the Magician** reveals the Magician tab. A tab you have not earned does not appear at all.
2. **Every quest has its marker and its completion moment.** The rule is general (Pell's old "A First Step" stub is gone). Every quest completion plays the **QUEST COMPLETE banner and sound**, then the **rewards screen**. Intro steps that give a small gift show the rewards screen too.
3. **New start.**
   - **Welcome to Meadow Town** is one quest of eleven short steps, all in town: Pell (talking, J, L, markers; 5 Copper) → Mara (shop; 2 Blueberry) → buy a Strawberry → Mara (bag) → put a berry on Q and eat it → Mara's chest → put an item in and take it back (1 Meadow Dew) → Ila (families, essence, the bench; 1 Clear Dew) → Tamsin (character sheet) → spend your first point (2 Strawberry) → Calden's hand-off. Ends with 10 XP. No materials.
   - **Path of the Warrior** (Calden): talk, land 3 basic attacks on the post, talk, defeat 5 Field Mice and gather 5 Twig, return. Completing it reveals the Warrior tab and opens **Power Strike training**; it is **not** granted.
   - **Path of the Magician** (Tamsin): starts when Power Strike is trained (she shows `!`). Bring 6 Wool and 4 Feather. Reveals the Magician tab and opens Magic Bolt training.
   - These replace The Weight of a Swing, The Hero's Paths and The Quiet Spell. **Old saves** that finished them get Welcome, both Path quests complete, and keep their skills.
   - **Quests appear gradually**: A Warm Coat and Beyond the Pasture Gate at level 3 after the Warrior Path; The Bargain of Rage at level 4; Roots That Grow at level 4 after Beyond the Pasture Gate; What the Banks Hide at level 5 after it; The Cracked Stone (Calden `!`, level 5 after Beyond the Pasture Gate, or a Cracked Bank Stone picked up after it); Ember in the Scale (level 6 after The Cracked Stone, or a Smoldering Scale after it). The STR gates stay on the turn-in steps. The Dirt Path no longer needs a passive point.
4. **NPCs never supply quest materials.** The Hero's Paths kit, Tamsin's Magic Bolt kit and Ila's Nature kit are gone. Ila now asks the player to gather the first rank's cost (Nature Essence 20, Pebble 16, Bark 12, 18 Copper) and plant it themselves; the tracker shows the counts. A test checks that no conversation adds materials.
5. **Much smaller rewards**, exactly the table in the plan (for example Path of the Warrior 25 XP and 5 Copper; Hot Edge 45 XP, 8 Copper, 3 Elemental Essence, Flame Cleave training).
6. **Quest copy.** Every title, journal line and conversation is rewritten to the tone guide (Pell warm, Mara dry, Calden gruff, Ila growing things, Tamsin counting), with a start, a "not yet" and a turn-in for each. New titles: Beyond the Pasture Gate, The Bargain of Rage, Roots That Grow, Wool Before Winter, What the Banks Hide, The Cracked Stone, Ember in the Scale.
7. **Back as well as Continue.** Every multi-page conversation has a Back button (hidden on the first page). Enter or click continues, Backspace or ← goes back, Esc still closes. Going back never repeats or undoes a step.
8. **Magic scales with INT** exactly as the basic attack and Warrior Path skills scale with STR (+1 per 2 points). STR does nothing for Magic Bolt, Fire Bolt, Thorn Volley and Chain Spark; INT does nothing for Power Strike. Mana Shield is unchanged. The character screen shows **Attack** and **Magic attack**.
9. **The journal shows only startable quests.** Active, completed, and a quest that can be started right now. No "soon" entries and no teasers.
10. **Basic attack: a gray side slash.** A wide horizontal crescent in front of the Hero: a crisp white-gray edge, a softer gray body and a short tail that fades fast, reaching the 70 px melee range. Damage, hit-stop, sparks and crit shake fire from inside the animation, on its impact frame.
11. **Drops.** Every drop chance is lowered by 25% (gear, rares and Copper included), and nothing but Copper drops more than 3 from one kill (for example Twig 2–5 is now 2–3 at 30%, not 40%). Copper keeps its amounts. Guaranteed quest drops stay guaranteed.
12. **Slots.** Buffs (Dig In, Rage, Mana Shield) go only in the 2 buff slots, attacks only in the 4 attack slots, potions only on Q and G. A wrong slot is refused with a hint ("Buffs go in the buff slots"). Saves with a skill in the wrong slot are sorted on load.

## Judgment calls

- **Intro shape.** The plan's table has seven rows; I split the actions (buy, eat from Q, chest round trip, spend a point) from the conversations so each is detected for real, which gives eleven steps. The chest conversation is Mara's, and the chest opens after it.
- **Warrior Path steps.** I gave the post and the mice their own "return to Calden" lines, so it is five steps: talk, post, talk, mice and Twig, return.
- **Magician Path** has three steps: talk, bring the items (it moves on by itself once the bag has them), return.
- **The Cracked Stone** now starts by talking to Calden like Ember in the Scale, and the first Burrow Mole killed at level 5+ after Beyond the Pasture Gate always drops a stone (once per character), so it can never be missed.
- **Rewards screen for gifts** is titled "A small gift" rather than "Complete".
- The completion banner plays for about a second, then the rewards screen opens.
- Old saves are recognised by having any progress (the old quests, a level above 1, or a skill) and get the intro marked done.

## Checks

`npm run build` is clean and `npm run smoke` passes with no console errors. The smoke test was rewritten around a fresh Hero: it plays the whole intro chain with real key presses and clicks (including Back / Continue, the banner and every gift screen), both Path quests, the gradual appearance of the rest, hidden trees and tabs, no kits, the slot rules, old-save migration, INT versus STR, and the drop cap over 630 simulated kills.
