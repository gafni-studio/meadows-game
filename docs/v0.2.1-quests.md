Skills are locked until their line is done, then trained at Calden from rank 1. New saves start with an empty skill bar (potions in 5 and 6 only). Old saves that already had `power_strike` (even at rank 0) are migrated: the line is marked done and the skill is set to rank 1.

Named foes (`respawn_s = 0`) spawn only while their kill-step is active and do not come back after the kill. Sprite aliases: Wool-Shoulder → wool sheep, Bank-Cracker → burrow mole, Cinderback → ember beetle, with a gold-ish tint.

## Root Lash and Dig In

No quest. Learned at Calden when the sheet gates are met (Root Lash lv4 + STR 6; Dig In lv5 + STR 7).

- **Root Lash:** 145% line attack, 10 MP, ~2s cooldown, +12% damage per scaling rank. Knocks back non-bosses.
- **Dig In:** 0.5s window at rank 1 (+0.05s per scaling rank from r2), +100% defense (2×), knockback-immune, 2 MP, ~4s cooldown.

Mixed recipes are on `skill_costs`: Root Lash r2 adds 12 Bark (pasture); Dig In r2 adds 16 Twig. Earthbreaker r2 lists Twig 40 (overrides the scaled 56) plus 20 Dry Root. A rank-r row that names a rank-1 material **overrides** the scaled quantity; other rank-r rows are additional.

## Assumptions (feel, not on the sheet)

Root Lash knockback 900 / 400ms stun; Dig In cooldown 4s; named-mob speeds 13–15 and higher knockback resistance.
