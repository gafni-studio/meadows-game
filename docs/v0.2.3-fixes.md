# v0.2.3 — playtest fixes

Twenty-one notes from a playthrough of v0.2.2. This is a polish pass; no new zone or questline.

Live after deploy: https://meadows-gafni.netlify.app

## What changed

1. **Basic knockback halved.** A normal F attack now pushes about half as far (210, was 420). Power Strike, Earthbreaker, Flame Cleave, and Root Lash keep the big knockback.

2. **Mob movement speed restored.** v0.1.3 had slowed walking together with the animation. Only the animation was too fast, so movement is back to the v0.1.2 pace (mouse 28, sheep 20, crow 38; Dirt Banks mobs scaled ×1.4 to match). Walk and swing animations are unchanged (Crow `anim_speed` 1, others 0.5; hero walk still ~35% slower). Player speed stays 148.

3. **Click selects, it does not use.** Clicking a bag or equipped tile highlights it and shows Use / Equip / Unequip / Drop / hotbar buttons in the info box. Enter still uses or equips from the keyboard.

4. **Keyboard hints are quieter.** The top-right help and the in-window hint line are smaller and dimmer.

5. **“Sellable junk” is just “Junk”.**

6. **Inventory tabs.** All / Gear / Consumables / Materials / Junk. Bag tiles are smaller so the info box has more room. Empty slots only appear on All.

7. **Gear comparison.** Selecting a bag piece shows Attack and Defense against what you are wearing, e.g. `Attack 13 (+2)` in green or red. Equipped pieces are not compared to themselves. Affixes are included.

8. **Wider patrols.** Idle mobs wander up to 16 tiles from their spawn (was 4) and stand still less often, clamped to the map.

9. **Quest skills stay hidden** until that quest is discovered (started or finished). Once visible they stay locked until the quest is done, then you train them at Calden.

10. **Softer pickup sound.** A quiet two-note chime (WebAudio), not a sharp beep.

11. **Attacks feel more responsive.** A swing pressed during recovery is buffered and fires as soon as you can act. The sword arc is smoother and the body lunge no longer leaves a stuck scale.

12. **Power Strike costs 20 Monster Essence** at rank 1 (was 30). Rank 2 scales from that: 32 essence, 8 scrolls, 32 copper.

13. **Mara sells a Short Sword** for 30 copper. A plain step up from the Wooden Sword (mid 13 × 1.1). One-line desc: “A short iron blade, sturdier than a practice sword.” It is at the end of the shop list so existing navigation is unchanged.

14. **Flat attack from the main stat.** Every 2 STR (INT for Mage, AGI for Rogue) adds +1 attack after the weapon: `floor(weapon × multiplier) + floor(main_stat / 2)`. At 0 STR a Wooden Sword is still 11. The formula is hidden from the character sheet (no “+1% damage”).

15. **Rage tint only while Rage is active.** The red tint is driven by the live buff every frame. Buff and cooldown timers use the wall clock, so travelling or restarting a scene cannot leave a leftover tint or a frozen timer.

16. **Quest complete** shows a “QUEST COMPLETE” banner and plays a short chime, on top of the usual toast.

17. **A Crack in the Dirt and Hot Edge only unlock training.** They no longer grant Earthbreaker or Flame Cleave at rank 1. You still get the XP, coins, and item rewards, then you pay Calden to learn rank 1. The Weight of a Swing and Blood Up still grant Power Strike and Rage at rank 1. Existing saves that already have the skill keep it.

18. **On-screen objectives tracker.** A clearer panel lists each active quest and its current objective. **L** shows or hides it (L was free after the journal moved to J). The choice is remembered. The top-right help mentions it.

19. **Item tooltips** no longer show roll ranges or “Sells for.” The shop still shows the buy price. Owned counts stay.

20. **Wild Apple is retired** from the shop and from drops (kept so old bags still work). **Strawberry** restores 18 HP, **Blueberry** restores 15 MP. Each costs 5 copper in the shop. Field Mouse drops Strawberry at 6%, Wool Sheep drops Blueberry at 6% (the apple was 4%). Crow and the Old Stump no longer drop a fruit. Short descs: “A ripe meadow strawberry.” / “A small, tart blueberry.”

21. **Wool-Shoulder always drops 5–15 copper**, rolled in on-kill so a drop-rate bonus cannot multiply it (same idea as Live Ember).

22. **Lower skill MP costs.** Power Strike 5 (was 8), Rage 10 (was 12), Earthbreaker 10 (was 14), Flame Cleave 14 (was 20), Soul Guard 18 (was 26). Soul Guard is data-only and stays parked. Root Lash and Dig In are unchanged.

23. **Higher copper drop chance** on Crows and Dirt Banks regulars. Crow, Burrow Mole, and Earth Sprite go from 60% to 80%; Ember Beetle 64% to 82%; Spark Bug (already in the sheet, content in v0.2.4) 50% to 80%. Quantity bands (1–4) are unchanged. Named quest bosses have no copper drop-table row; Wool-Shoulder’s 5–15 copper is still the guaranteed on-kill.

24. **Practice post hits like a mob.** You can strike it from normal melee reach (about 70px in front, same window as monsters), not only while standing on it. A real damage number (with crit colour) floats up on every hit — basic attacks and skills — instead of a “THUD.” Quest dummy swings still only count basic F hits.

25. **Skill cooldown queue.** Pressing a skill that is on cooldown queues one activation, which fires when the cooldown ends. Pressing the same skill again while it is queued does nothing (no banner). The “on cooldown” banner only appears on the first press, and only when more than 5 seconds remain (so Rage’s 60s cooldown still tells you; Power Strike’s 1s does not).

## Judgment calls

- Berries are only on Mouse and Sheep, as asked. Crow and Old Stump lost the apple and did not get a berry.
- Strawberry 18 HP and Blueberry 15 MP sit just above the old apple (15 HP) and well below Meadow Dew (40) / Clear Dew (30). Shop price 5 copper, same as the apple.
- Short Sword mid 13 × 1.1 sits between Wooden Sword (10 × 1.1 = 11) and Meadow Blade (14 × 1.15). Shop-only, not a drop.
- Dirt Banks walk speeds were designed after the v0.1.3 slowdown, so they were scaled by 28/20 = 1.4 rather than copied from pasture numbers.
- Incoming hit knockback (when a mob hits you) is unchanged. Only your basic attack was halved.
- Attack interval is unchanged; the input buffer and smoother swing are what address the “clunky / stuck” feel.
- Completing Crack or Hot on an old save that already has rank 1 does not take the skill away.
- Copper chance only (not quantity) was raised, by about +20 percentage points, so Crows and Banks mobs drop coins clearly more often than the pasture mouse/sheep (70%) without becoming guaranteed. Spark Bug’s rate is set now so v0.2.4 does not need a second pass. Named quest bosses were left without a copper drop-table (they never had one); Wool-Shoulder keeps the guaranteed 5–15 on-kill.
- The practice-post interact / “arrive here” radius is unchanged (you still walk up to it to press E). Only the attack hitbox grew to melee range.
- Cooldown banners for short skills (Power Strike 1s, Flame Cleave 2.5s, Earthbreaker 3s, Dig In 4s, Root Lash 2s) are suppressed; only Rage (60s) and Soul Guard (30s, parked) show one, and only on the first press.
