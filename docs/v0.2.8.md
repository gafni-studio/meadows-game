# v0.2.8 — Magician Path

The second Path, built on v0.2.7's tree UI, from `planning/v0.2.8.md`. A ranged, MP-hungry path with five skills, a tutorial from a new town NPC, and projectile skills made to feel good. No new area. Sheet patch: `scripts/sheet-patches/v0.2.8.py`.

## The chain

```
Magic Bolt (basic, root)
  └── Mana Shield (basic buff)
        ├── Fire Bolt (Elemental)
        └── Thorn Volley (Nature)
              └── Chain Spark (Elemental)
```

- Renames of the four unused mage skills (ids, names, recipe rows and icons): Energy Blast → **Magic Bolt**, Energy Shield → **Mana Shield**, Firebolt → **Fire Bolt**, Thorn Bind → **Thorn Volley**. **Chain Spark** is new. Holy Light is still parked. Frost Shard, Spirit Drain and other Spirit skills are **not** implemented (the tree shows two dashed future slots).
- Any Hero can train the path; each skill needs the previous one at rank 1+; no level gate.
- **Costs MP.** Magic Bolt 8, Mana Shield 14, Fire Bolt 16, Thorn Volley 14, Chain Spark 20 (Power Strike is 5, Flame Cleave 14), so MP potions (Q / G) and regen matter more.
- **Recipes** are explicit at every rank 1–10, existing Meadow and Dirt Banks goods only, 10–40 per material, the family essence as the main cost: Monster Essence for the basics, Elemental Essence for Fire Bolt and Chain Spark (plus Fire and Lightning Shards), Nature Essence for Thorn Volley (plus Dry Root, Bark, Clay). Earth Crystal at ranks 5 and 10. Deep beats spreading: each skill scales per rank (basic +5%, Nature +12%, Elemental +10% damage per rank) on top of its base.

## How the skills fight

- **Magic Bolt** (130%): a white-blue energy bolt with a short mote trail.
- **Mana Shield**: cuts incoming damage by **30%, +5% per rank** (max 60%) for 45 s, against swings, pebbles and bumps alike. A dome with sparkle appears when cast and a gently pulsing ward stays on the Hero until the buff ends.
- **Fire Bolt** (190%): an orange orb with embers, a **burn** on top (same burn as Flame Cleave), and the fire bloom on impact. Elemental, so Weak on beasts, Immune on elemental mobs.
- **Thorn Volley** (3 × 70%): three green thorns in a slight fan, staggered 70 ms apart, each rolling its own hit. Nature.
- **Chain Spark** (150%): a cyan spark that, on hit, leaps to up to three more foes within 150 px, each jump 110 ms later and 25% weaker, with a crackling lightning link between targets. Elemental.
- **Projectiles** fly visibly (about 410–540 px/s), hit the first foe they touch, and **fizzle at 320 px**. Damage, impact effect, hit-stop and sparks all land at the moment of arrival, not on the key press. Affinity applies exactly as for the Warrior skills.

## Tamsin and The Quiet Spell

A new Meadow Town NPC, **Tamsin** (a dreamy, exact, counting sort of magician, with a blue-hatted sprite made from Ila's). She starts after Calden's tutorial, so the Paths UI has already been seen. Six short pages cover the second Path, the chain, mana, and which family essence pays for what. She hands over the Magic Bolt rank 1 materials; then: open the Magician tab (K, then Tab or click), train Magic Bolt (it auto-equips to an attack slot), and report back. Rewards 70 XP, 20 Copper, **Clear Dew ×4** (a reason to care about MP) and Monster Essence ×6. Afterwards E at Tamsin opens the Paths window on the Magician tab.

## Tree UI

The Magician tab is now a real tree (it was "coming soon"): five nodes, connecting lines, family colours, locked / available / trained, next-rank cost. Mana Shield lands in a buff slot (5–6), the attacks in 1–4, and a Hero can mix both paths' skills in the same slots.

## Judgment calls

- Mana Shield is a plain damage reduction (30%+), not a reflect or a flat-defense buff like the old Energy Shield, to read clearly next to Dig In. Its numbers live in the sheet text and `assumptions.ts` (`BOLT`).
- Fire Bolt, Thorn Volley and Chain Spark are classed Elemental / Nature / Elemental for affinity, so they are Immune against the Banks' elemental mobs (as Flame Cleave already is); Magic Bolt is neutral and works on everything.
- Tamsin stands at tile 32 between Ila and Pell. I recoloured Ila's sprite for her; it is placeholder art.
- The Chain Spark icon is a copy of the (unused) Lightning Dash icon until there is art for it.
