# v0.2.4 — The Dirt Path

A first mixed hunt past the east pasture, and two new regulars in the Dirt Banks. Warrior only. Soul Guard, the Meadow Shrine, and infusion stay parked. v0.2.3 combat rules are unchanged (original mob movement pace, halved basic knockback, flat STR).

## In plain words

- After you spend a first passive point (Ila's trees, T), **The Dirt Path** appears in the journal. Talk to **Pell** at the east gate. He sends you to clear **4 Crows** at the east end of Sunny Pasture, walk the dirt path into the **Dirt Banks**, then hunt **5 Burrow Moles and 3 Ember Beetles** and come back. He pays **120 XP, 25 Copper, Nature Essence ×8, Elemental Essence ×5, and Meadow Dew ×2**. No skill is granted — a taste of the banks, not a skipped rank.
- **Spark Bug** and **Frost Moth** now live in the Dirt Banks (three of each, with the moles, sprites, and beetles). Spark Bugs favor the warmer east with the Ember Beetles; Frost Moths favor the cooler west with the moles. They share the banks' materials (Dry Root, Tough Root, Earth Grit, Warm Stone) and each leave a unique extra: **Spark Shell** and **Pale Wing**, sell-only junk. Warrior gear from them is rare, about one piece every 150–200 kills. Monster Essence drops more often than Elemental Essence.
- The Meadow Shrine stays closed. These two were drawn for it later; they overlap here for now.

## The Dirt Path (B-001)

| Step | What you do | How it advances |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Talk to Pell at the east gate | Starts once a first passive point is spent. Talking to Pell sends you out. |
| 2 | Defeat 4 Crows in the east of Sunny Pasture | A hunt count (not a named foe). Auto-advances at 4. The tracker shows e.g. Crow 2/4. |
| 3 | Take the dirt path into the Dirt Banks | Auto-advances when you enter the area. |
| 4 | Defeat 5 Burrow Moles and 3 Ember Beetles, then return to Pell | Hunt counts, then talk to Pell to complete. |

**Prereq.** The nine-quest starter chain is still only stubbed (talking to Pell still grants "A First Step" once). The real gate is a first passive point: the `sq_first_passive` flag, any passive rank ≥ 1, or a pending upgrade. Spending that point sets the flag and auto-starts the quest at step 1 ("Talk to Pell"). Existing saves that already have a passive rank pick the quest up on load.

**Rewards** (from the last step only): 120 XP, 25 Copper, `nature_essence x8; elemental_essence x5; meadow_dew x2`. `unlock_skill` is empty. Completing does not grant or unlock a skill.

## Spark Bug and Frost Moth

Both already existed on the sheet as level-6 Elemental regulars (Spark aggressive, Moth passive) with CC0 art. They now spawn in `dirt_banks`:

- Spawn counts: `burrow_mole 6, earth_sprite 4, ember_beetle 4, spark_bug 3, frost_moth 3` (20 regulars on 114 tiles).
- Zones: Spark Bug `[55, 108]` east with beetles; Frost Moth `[12, 52]` west with moles.
- Movement: Spark Bug 25, Frost Moth 23 — in the restored v0.1.2 / v0.2.3 Banks pace (default 24, Banks ≈ ×1.4 of a slower draft). Animation stays at `anim_speed` 0.5.
- Feel: Spark is snappy (acc 12, eva 11, kbRes 0.2, windup 260–520 ms). Moth is floaty (acc 11, eva 13, kbRes 0.1, windup 320–600 ms).
- They remain listed on the Meadow Shrine row for later. The Shrine is not opened.

## Judgment calls

- **Frost Moth** is the optional second Banks mob: already in the sheet and art, elemental contrast to Spark Bug, fits a 3–6 Banks as a deeper-end hunt. Level 6 in a 3–6 zone is intentional.
- **Shrine stays closed.** The two are still listed on that row for a later pass; spawning them in the Banks now is an overlap, not a shrine opening.
- **Starter chain.** Do not require the unplayable nine starter quests. A first passive point is the real prereq; the flag is set when it is spent. Auto-start into "Talk to Pell" so the journal and tracker have something to show.
- **Hunt counts** are a new mechanic (not named single kills). Mid-hunt steps auto-advance; the last hunt waits for Pell. The tracker shows `Crow 2/4` style progress.
- **Gear trim.** Spark Bug and Frost Moth already had rogue/mage weapons that would have pushed the pool out of the 150–200 band if warrior pieces were stacked on top. Those were cut. A 0.06% worn_staff on the moth is a tiny mage overlap; warrior-only is 0.62% (1 in 161), total 0.68% (1 in 147), treated as ≈150.
- **Leftover junk** on those drop tables was not cleaned out; this pass only adds shared mats, uniques, and the retuned gear/essence/copper.
- **Frost copper 80** because it is a Banks regular now (v0.2.3 note 23 applied here).
- Soul Guard, Shrine, infusion, Spiritual, and Hunter's Luck stay parked. No new class besides Warrior.

## v0.2.3 rules still in force

Hero walk animation ~35% slower (`ANIM.heroWalkPeriod` 95). Crow `anim_speed` 1, others 0.5. Mob **movement** is the restored v0.1.2 pace (mouse 28, sheep 20, crow 38, default 24; Dirt Banks ×1.4). Basic knockback 210; specials keep the large values. Attack is `floor(weapon×mult) + floor(mainStat/2) + cheats.atk` (every 2 STR = +1 attack; the formula is hidden). Player speed 148.
